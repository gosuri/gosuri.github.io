## Summary

Rob Frankel's "The Biz Call" runs Greg through a structured founder questionnaire (recorded 2021-04-28). Greg describes Akash as "the world's first and only decentralized open source cloud," leveraging ~85% underutilized capacity across 8.4 million data centers, with tenants seeing 8-10x lower costs than Amazon/Google/Microsoft. Revenue model: "tokenized open source software" — a take-rate on transactions distributed to token holders, with Overclock Labs earning via its token holdings. He recaps AngelHack/Firebase, the deployment-friction origin story, and differentiators beyond cost: unstoppability ("no one can stop you from using Akash, including myself — I cannot take it down"), deplatforming insurance/disaster recovery for businesses, and extensibility. Asked about the exit strategy, he answers: acquire the trillion-dollar cloud market, seeing "no reason" not to reach 100% penetration. The standout segment is his long-term vision of edge devices earning income: Tesla cars as "giant data centers on wheels" with ~200 computers whose GPU capacity idles during overnight charging and could be sold to AI researchers — while ~20% of AI companies' margins go to Amazon as an "invisible tax." Company facts circa April 2021: profitable, 17 employees (targeting 35 by year-end), double-digit monthly growth, ~1 hour to first deployment (goal: 10 minutes), growth led by Molly Lee. He closes announcing "the world's first open marketplace for GPU compute" coming later that year, citing customers spending $1-2M/month on GPUs who want to move to Akash.
