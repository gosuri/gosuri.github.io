## Summary

A Q&A clip where Greg explains the relationship between Overclock Labs and Akash Network. Overclock Labs is a for-profit San Francisco company founded in 2015 "to make multi-cloud deployments a reality," bringing then-new Kubernetes to market; while deploying, they discovered most data-center compute sat unused, and "Akash was a solution to a problem Overclock Labs had." Akash Network is an independent network beyond Overclock: other companies (Cache/Quasarch, Praetor, Moultrie per garbled captions) contribute, most GitHub contributors are outside Overclock, and a Decentralized Cloud Foundation entity exists for governance functions. Overclock is privately funded and — notably, in the 2022 bear market — he states they have enough capital "to last this next four years or so."
