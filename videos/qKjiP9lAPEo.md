## Summary

A Frictionless Podcast interview with Multicoin Capital co-founder Kyle Samani covering DePIN investing lessons (favor passive supply-side participation, don't over-engineer verification, lead your community), the heterogeneity of decentralized compute markets, Render, his Filecoin reversal, and his integrated-vs-modular blockchain thesis favoring Solana. Greg Osuri does not appear and Akash is never mentioned; relevant to this archive only as a contemporaneous investor perspective on decentralized compute (e.g., Samani's view that training over the public internet "is not going to fly ever" while inference can be split up, and that software will conform to hardware because the market is GPU-constrained).
