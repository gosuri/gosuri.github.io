## Summary

An "Ask Akash" Q&A clip on how fiat payments (via partner Bixelo/"Vixelo" per captions) affect AKT. The speaker (uncredited; consistent with Greg's voice in this series) explains fiat simplifies onboarding for the non-crypto world used to net-30 invoices with Amazon and Google, since acquiring AKT via exchanges/KYC is hard for a credit-card customer. He claims Akash is "still like 95 percent cheaper than your traditional cloud" with ~50 providers currently, and expects provider incentives to grow that to 1,000-1,500 providers "very soon." Fiat won't undermine the token: the payment partner must buy AKT off-market to deploy, because "protocols cannot support fiat payments... the only way to use Akash deployment platform is to use AKT" — even planned multi-currency/stable settlement roots down to AKT.
