## Summary

Keynote at Akash Accelerate 2025 by Illia Polosukhin (Near co-founder, transformer paper co-author) — Greg Osuri does not speak in this video. Illia argues that whoever controls AGI controls everything it touches, and proposes "user-owned AI" built on decentralized confidential machine learning: trusted execution environments (Nvidia Hopper/Blackwell, Intel TDX) that make model weights encrypted and interactions confidential and verifiable, enabling model monetization without a centralized serving company, plus crypto-economic benchmark competitions to sustain a decentralized AI-lab flywheel. He references Greg's earlier remarks at the event in passing ("Greg mentioned a bunch of them") on energy consumption and global latency challenges of centralized AI, but does not quote him directly.
