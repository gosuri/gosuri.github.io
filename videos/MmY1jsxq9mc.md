## Summary

Conference talk by Prof. Sriram Vishwanath (UT Austin) at an Akash event. He argues decentralized AI — especially federated learning — is not merely cheaper than centralized AI but the only viable solution for domains like healthcare (privacy, hyperlocality, regulation blocking any scalable centralized foundation model) and 6G (privacy, bandwidth, locality, latency), and announces plans to team with Akash and target roughly two dozen startups in the space. Greg Osuri does not speak; he is mentioned only in passing ("I've already promised Jang and Greg").
