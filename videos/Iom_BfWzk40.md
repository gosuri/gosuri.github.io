## Summary

A monthly Akash Weekly community call (Twitter Spaces) hosted by community events manager Nadia Bajuelo, featuring Greg Osuri's July 2022 recap and a product segment with head of product Anil Murty on IP Leases. Greg highlights community members profitably running incentivized-testnet validators (NEAR Stake Wars, Quicksilver) on Akash at a fraction of hyperscaler cost, calling bear markets a great time for such activity. He reports peak network usage of ~11 TB of actively leased memory and 29% utilization, contrasting it with Filecoin's 0.07% to argue Akash has genuine product-market fit before any supply-side incentives kick in — his recurring thesis that incentives should come post-PMF. He is notably excited about the upcoming Ethereum merge freeing an enormous pool of GPUs that Akash could onboard to cut cloud GPU costs, and about Cosmos interchain accounts letting DAOs on Juno deploy directly to Akash. He also argues the industry must decouple Web3's image from DeFi (post-Celsius/Voyager collapse) by showcasing infrastructure like decentralized cloud. Anil Murty explains IP Leases (publicly routable static IPs, analogous to AWS Elastic IP, but monetizable by providers), its role in node discoverability without centralized coordination, and its edge-net testing status. AMA topics include credit-card payments for deployments via the new authorized-spend feature (avoiding KYC/custody issues) and Overclock Labs' runway ("well capitalized for the next two to three years"). Dated elements: pre-merge Ethereum GPU speculation, mainnet 3 features, hiring push.

## Predictions & Notable Claims

### [gpu-economics] ETH merge will flood Akash with cheap GPUs
> "That's that Ethereum network in itself attracted, right, so 19 billion dollars of GPUs available means we should be able to be in a good position to leverage those GPUs onto Akash Network, thereby reducing the GPU cost on the cloud significantly. So we can't really wait for the ETH merge to happen, and we'll have the GPU capability by then."
> — [00:11:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Iom_BfWzk40&t=688s)

**Context:** Discussing a Messari article positioning Akash as a destination for the huge GPU supply that Ethereum's proof-of-stake merge (Sept 2022) would idle; Greg says GPU support is a "big dream" they are racing to ship.

### [crypto-depin] Incentives should come after product-market fit, or supply outstrips demand
> "Tokens don't last forever and tokens usually need a cap, or else you end up in a Terra-like situation... it's really good to see strong product market fit signaling before the incentives kick in, because that'll allow us to grow faster and grow quickly."
> — [00:09:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Iom_BfWzk40&t=558s)

**Context:** Contrasting Akash's 29% memory utilization with Filecoin's 0.07% to argue over-incentivizing node supply pre-PMF leaves nothing to incentivize demand later; a recurring Akash tokenomics thesis.

### [crypto-depin] Web3 must decouple from finance and showcase infrastructure
> "It's very important for us to decouple finance from Web3 technologies and showcase these technologies like Akash and like storage technologies... and infrastructure technologies such as libp2p and whatnot."
> — [00:13:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Iom_BfWzk40&t=816s)

**Context:** Reacting to the Celsius/Voyager collapses inviting "bad regulation"; Greg argues Web2 engineers care about deeply useful tech like cloud, not finance, so infrastructure must become Web3's brand.

### [cloud-decentralization] DAOs will deploy cloud workloads directly via smart contracts
> "By enabling interchain accounts, an application on Juno can deploy directly on Akash using a Juno account and not an Akash account... now you're going to see DAOs, members of DAOs, are directly deploying onto Akash using a smart contract from Juno, which is nothing like we ever saw in Ethereum. These are the kind of use cases that are going to showcase Cosmos and the power of Cosmos to the rest of the ecosystem."
> — [00:14:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Iom_BfWzk40&t=861s)

**Context:** Announcing that Cosmos interchain-accounts support is code-complete and in review on GitHub; Greg predicts smart-contract-driven cloud deployment as a showcase Cosmos use case.
