## Summary

A panel at Akash's own Accelerate 2025 event featuring Casey (Topology fund, ex-Google/Paradigm), Jake Brukhman (CoinFund founder/CEO), and Ben Parr (Octane AI / Theory Forge Ventures), with an unnamed moderator. Greg Osuri does not speak — Ben Parr asks the room to applaud "Greg and Akash for bringing us all together." The panel argues decentralized AI has shifted from ideology to pragmatism: decentralized inference running ~90% cheaper than closed-source providers by aggregating spare compute (Casey cites a decentralized provider beating Google on a contract that week); Pluralis's lossless ~100x compression of inter-node training traffic enabling frontier-scale training on commodity GPUs over slow internet (Jake, noting big tech owns 90% of high-end compute but high-end is only ~2% of the world's GPUs); the Meta/Scale AI deal as proof you shouldn't train on a competitor's cloud (Ben). Other threads: co-owned model weights as an open-source monetization strategy, Prime Intellect's 32B decentralized-trained model and Gensyn's 60,000-participant RL swarm, predictions that decentralized training reaches ~100B parameters by year-end, the need for a "ChatGPT of decentralized AI" killer consumer app (Venice gets named), investor wishlists (DePIN-collected neural/brain data, verticalized AI, non-GPU infrastructure), and whether unstoppable community-owned AGI is a feature or a risk.
