## Summary

An Akash team explainer presented by Alani (not Greg) detailing the Polygon-Akash hackathon ("Polygonathon," Nov 22, 2021 - Feb 22, 2022): Polygon projects post migration jobs, community members migrate them to Akash hosting for rewards, and Akash matches up to 100 AKT per project. Covers the statement-of-work template, forum-based registration/submission, and judging criteria. Closes with boilerplate describing Akash as "the world's first decentralized and open source cloud" at "up to three times less the cost of centralized cloud providers."
