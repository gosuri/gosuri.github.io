## Summary

An "Ask Akash" Q&A clip where the host directs a community question to Greg by name: how can a beginner use the SDL builder? Greg answers as a hands-on developer — he builds apps regularly (the Akash ecosystem app, the feeds API) and currently copies old SDLs around, so a generator that produces an SDL from a form is great for new projects. He describes wanting the feeds API deployed across at least 10 regions using the builder's provider-metadata filters (IPs, locations, attributes). He's candid that the SDL builder won't help non-developers yet: "as a non-technical person you shouldn't even care about SDL — hopefully we will get to that point sometime in the future, but right now Akash is meant for developers, and SDL really makes a developer's life simple."
