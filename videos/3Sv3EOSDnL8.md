## Summary

TOKEN2049 Singapore 2024 main-stage talk, a sibling of Greg's deAI Summit and Cosmoverse keynotes from the same season. He argues crypto has "lost its way" and returns to first principles: the internet was born from decentralization (mainframes → PCs → hive-networked devices), but hyperscalers recentralized it — four companies now account for ~70% of IT spend, hardware vendors optimize for them, and the sovereignty gap is widening. Evidence tour: gated GPU access (Elon Musk's "GPUs harder to get than drugs" line, the Semafor story), the a16z "Trillion Dollar Paradox" cloud-tax study (50%+ of every online-service dollar goes to three clouds — Netflix, Asana, Palantir, even the DoD pay it), the CrowdStrike outage (7,000 Delta cancellations from one bad code push, with a warning about what a real state-actor attack would look like), railroad-era antitrust parallels, and AWS's 60%+ margins on a trillion-dollar business — "that's not okay" for something functioning as a public utility. The risk of hyper-concentration is "digital feudalism" where corporations ration who gets to do AI ("the spice must flow"). His alternative: compute as a public utility as free as air and water, delivered by DePIN networks — Akash (category creator, first decentralized supercloud, reverse-auction marketplace, 80% cheaper, four years without downtime), Filecoin, Bittensor. Adoption claims: Nvidia is Akash's biggest user and the only crypto protocol Nvidia uses; Nous Research, University of Texas, RIT, and Venice AI (which he uses personally); 50% quarter-over-quarter lease growth.

## Predictions & Notable Claims

### [cloud-decentralization] The cloud tax: 50% of every online-service dollar
> "The costs are getting out of control. Over 50% of every single penny we spend on online services goes to one of the three cloud providers. All of us pay a cloud tax without knowing who we [are] paying the tax to... even Department of Defense, which uses Palantir, clearly pays a cloud tax... Andreessen Horowitz did this study, it's called the Trillion Dollar Paradox."
> — [00:05:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3Sv3EOSDnL8&t=312s)

**Context:** Recurring quantified claim, here with the explicit a16z study name.

### [decentralized-ai] Digital feudalism: corporations rationing who gets to do AI
> "The real risk is us as a society going back to feudalism. I call this digital feudalism... the future is bleak where a corporation or a business dictates who gets to do AI, dictates a ration of resources to use AI. And we're kind of seeing that right now — you're seeing Amazon and Google of the world rationing computing power... we call [it] the spice, but the spice must flow."
> — [00:08:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3Sv3EOSDnL8&t=485s)

**Context:** His signature warning about hyper-concentration of compute power, with the Dune "spice" metaphor for GPUs.

### [cloud-decentralization] Compute should be as free as air and water
> "We believe compute should be a public utility. Compute should be as free as the air we breathe and water we drink, and that's the future we're all building towards... the access should be ungated, [it] should be open, [it] should be public."
> — [00:08:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3Sv3EOSDnL8&t=528s)

**Context:** The affirmative vision after the feudalism warning; ties to restoring sovereignty over data and compute.

### [gpu-economics] AWS's 60%+ margins are incompatible with a public utility
> "Amazon Web Services... alone [is] a trillion dollar business for Amazon, and profit margins are over 60%, and that's not okay. For example, the cost if you look at decentralized systems like Akash is 80% cheaper than what you would otherwise pay on Amazon, and that's because there's compute available everywhere."
