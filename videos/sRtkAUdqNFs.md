## Summary

Interview with host Miko Bits weeks before Akash's Mainnet 2 platform launch (Feb 2021). Greg pitches Akash as a peer-to-peer marketplace for cloud compute: permissionless (no signup/credit card), 2-3x — sometimes 10x — cheaper than AWS/Google/Microsoft, with deployments taking minutes instead of hours. He claims Akash was "the fastest growing cloud in the world" during testnet (3,800 deployments, ~480 active developers — more than Polkadot's monthly actives), and names Equinix — "the largest data center provider in the world," with ~100 regions and Amazon as its biggest customer — as the first exclusive provider for Mainnet 2. Technical discussion covers SDL (declarative deployment language), the reputation/attestation-based provider trust model (compared to Uniswap token lists), decoupled validator/provider roles, and the inflation-reward subsidy mechanism that "borrows from the future" to bridge bid-ask gaps. On the then-fresh Parler/AWS deplatforming, Greg gives a nuanced answer: censorship resistance and privacy are "foundational pillars for a liberal society," but free speech isn't free reach — Akash won't build censorship into the protocol, while individual providers remain responsible for and able to filter content. Forward-looking material includes Thorchain incentivizing validators to move off AWS onto Akash, "ownerless web services" (validators auto-paying for their own compute from commissions), an interoperable decentralized cloud stack (Graph, Handshake, NuCypher, Filecoin, Sia, Arweave) payable in any protocol token, running nodes locally on $100 RockPis, a decentralized services marketplace to counter cloud providers "stealing" open-source business models, and the royalty-bearing "Akash builders license" being developed with Brian Fox (Bash creator).

### [cloud-decentralization] Akash is 2-3x, sometimes 10x, cheaper than the big clouds

**Context:** Opening description of Akash, alongside "what takes normally tens of hours on the cloud takes minutes" and censorship resistance "becoming more and more important considering what's happening today" (Jan 2021).

### [cloud-decentralization] The fastest growing cloud in the world during testnet
> "During the testnet phase we were the fastest growing cloud in the world, with 3,800 deployments, about 480 active developers. To give you a context, Polkadot has about 420 monthly active developers."
> — [00:02:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sRtkAUdqNFs&t=132s)

**Context:** State-of-the-network answer before Mainnet 2 (platform launch dated Feb 14-15, 2021 in the conversation).

### [other] Equinix as Akash's first anchor provider
> "Our first biggest exclusive provider, and we're launching mainnet number two, is Equinix... Equinix is the largest data center provider in the world... they have 100 regions, so they're pretty well distributed... and their biggest customer I believe is Amazon."
> — [00:19:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sRtkAUdqNFs&t=1199s)

**Context:** Answer to how Akash bootstrapped baseline supply; earlier he argues going "directly to the source" (Equinix sells to Amazon at a premium) is one reason Akash is cheaper.

### [other] Censorship resistance and privacy are foundational pillars of a liberal society
> "This is the cost of liberty, right? I fundamentally believe censorship resistance and privacy are the foundational pillars for a liberal society."
> — [00:28:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sRtkAUdqNFs&t=1715s)

**Context:** Discussing the AWS/Parler deplatforming; he balances this with "free speech doesn't mean free reach" and notes providers on Akash remain responsible for content they host.

### [cloud-decentralization] 85-95% of compute is not being used
> "We began the company as an edge computing platform... that's when we discovered this is a massive opportunity of unused compute. When we were actually deploying this platform we noticed 85% or so of the compute is not being used — that's our conservative estimate. Some of our estimates are actually like 95% of the compute is not being used."
> — [00:34:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sRtkAUdqNFs&t=2099s)

**Context:** Origin story from 2015; the underutilization statistic is the foundational thesis behind Akash's supply model.

### [crypto-depin] Ownerless web services — infrastructure that pays for itself
> "What we really want to push further is ownerless web services, where say for example you're hosting a Thor node and you're a validator and you earn income as a validator — part of the commission can actually go to pay for compute itself from Akash without you touching an Akash wallet. That's really putting our foundation for a larger vision of an interoperable cloud."
> — [00:37:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sRtkAUdqNFs&t=2227s)

**Context:** Following the "deploy on Akash button on GitHub" idea; self-sustaining, non-custodial infrastructure as the endgame.

### [cloud-decentralization] An interoperable decentralized cloud with better UX than today's cloud
> "...interoperable world, where the user experience is going to be much better than what you'll experience on the cloud today, and we are working with NuCypher and Filecoin to make this happen."
> — [00:39:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sRtkAUdqNFs&t=2354s)

**Context:** Predicting SDL will orchestrate Graph, Handshake, NuCypher, Filecoin, Sia, and Arweave together, payable in any protocol's token ("I think we're going to see a very interoperable world" begins in the prior caption block).

### [local-compute] Hosting decentralized service nodes locally on $100 hardware
