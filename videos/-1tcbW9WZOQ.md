## Summary

Community livestream hosted by Adam Wozney a week after the Osmosis DEX launch, with Greg Osuri and Osmosis co-founder Sunny Aggarwal. Updates: Bittrex Global and BHEX listings, and Akash's selection to support Helium's new blockchain validators. Greg reports ~17% week-over-week growth in total deployments three months after mainnet 2, and celebrates a self-serving loop: Osmosis's API servers and nodes run on Akash, and Osmosis validators/developers can swap OSMO for AKT on the DEX to pay for their compute — "pure OSMO," no fiat touched. He cites ~$13M AKT liquidity with under 2% slippage on a $50K trade — better than any centralized exchange ever provided — and argues DEXes are crypto's most efficient liquidity mechanism. He explains the chicken-and-egg problem for non-ERC20 tokens getting exchange listings and calls Osmosis a paradigm shift that ends the liquidity-vs-usability trade-off for appchain builders (mentioning Sentinel dVPN and Regen — "I'm going to ape into it"). Sunny recounts the Osmosis launch story (Sikka, Chainapsis/Keplr merger, threshold-encryption front-running resistance origins), launch-week chaos, IBC UX work (asset lists), liquidity bootstrapping pools (Regen's LBP), and the roadmap: curve-v2-style concentrated liquidity, front-running resistance as a killer feature, and superfluid staking. On stablecoins, Sunny praises the UST design; Greg says UST on Osmosis would enable stable-currency payments for Akash (notable in hindsight given UST's 2022 collapse). Sunny predicts Gravity DEX's batch-execution model migrates to Osmosis ("a DEX doesn't belong on the Cosmos Hub") and defends slow Ethereum bridging with a Hamiltonian-protectionism analogy that Greg endorses. Ends with community Q&A, Handshake DNS for Osmosis, and geography-fact banter.

### [crypto-depin] A self-serving, fiat-free cloud economy is live
> "Osmosis API servers and nodes are actually running on Akash, so it's a self-serving ecosystem at this point... you can actually pay for your compute using OSMO tokens by exchanging them on the DEX to AKT and using their AKT to pay for your compute."

**Context:** Greg calls this "the first sort of self-fulfilling" loop — "pure OSMO... no touching fiat" — and predicts more of it: Sentinel dVPN nodes and Persistence hosts running on Akash paid entirely through IBC.

### [crypto-depin] DEXes are the most efficient liquidity mechanism in crypto
> "None of the centralized exchanges could actually provide [that] kind of liquidity to us, and that's a testament on why a decentralized exchange is the most efficient liquidity provisioning mechanism in crypto."

**Context:** After citing ~$13M of AKT liquidity on Osmosis and under 2% slippage on a $50K transaction — "the best liquidity we had in history."

### [crypto-depin] Osmosis ends the liquidity-vs-usability trade-off for appchains
> "Osmosis is a paradigm shift that the developers no longer need to make that trade-off... soon enough the bridge is going to get built between ERC20 and the Cosmos ecosystem... it's very easy to see the Osmosis just becoming a massive liquidity provider very, very soon."

**Context:** Greg explaining that pre-Osmosis, token builders had to choose between ERC20 liquidity and native-chain usability — "I find it very hard that you will use a ERC20 token to deploy something... because of the gas cost, which is ridiculous."
