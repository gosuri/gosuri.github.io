## Summary

An Akash team explainer presented by Alani (not Greg) introducing Testnet 3 and its four headline features: persistent storage (data survives across deployment restarts within a lease, with cross-lease persistence planned), fractional AKT/uAKT (removing the one-uAKT-per-block minimum so light workloads like a wallet or personal blog stay cheap even if token price triples), authz (authorized spend from another wallet restricted to deployment activity, enabling teams and faucetless onboarding), and an automatic inflationary-curve mechanism replacing manual governance-driven inflation corrections. Also covers the testnet signup/KYC workflow and the $200,000 prize pool.
