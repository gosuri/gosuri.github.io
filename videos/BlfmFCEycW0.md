## Summary

Live AMA hosted by Chris Zinke of Bittrex Global shortly after AKT's listing. Greg walks through his background (AngelHack, 150,000 developers in 50 cities; Firebase launched there; early Kubernetes contributor) and Akash's genesis: Overclock Labs built an edge-optimized cloud whose state-sync data structures "ended up looking like a merkle tree," so they adopted a real blockchain. He lays out the core thesis — tapping 8.2 million data centers running at ~85% underutilization via an open, permissionless marketplace where users set prices — and explains the "d-cloud" umbrella he coined (Akash compute, Sia/Filecoin storage, Handshake DNS, NuCypher key management), positioning Akash as "the gateway to the decentralized web." He differentiates Akash as the only live reputation-based (non-verifiable) decentralized compute platform that can run anything AWS runs, versus Golem/Sonm/iExec's verifiable compute. On DeFi he notes Serum's UI ran on Akash and argues front ends and middleware hosted on AWS are centralization and regulatory risk. Tokenomics: AKT secures the PoS chain, max supply 389M, decaying inflation shifting to fee-based rewards (up to 20% of network revenue to stakers). Roadmap items: persistent storage, a GPU marketplace to absorb Ethereum miners' soon-to-be-obsolete GPUs post-merge, cutting deploy time from 90 minutes to one minute, IBC/Osmosis/Gravity DEX and an Ethereum bridge. He is candid that d-web has no product-market fit yet, and repeatedly deflects hype about the "Supermini" GPU cluster device. Stats of the moment: 55 unique applications, 17x usage growth in two months, ~5,000 apps deployed.

## Predictions & Notable Claims

### [cloud-decentralization] Tapping 8.2 million underutilized data centers
> "The premise of Akash Network is to tap into 8.2 million data centers that remain underutilized — 85% or so capacity in those data centers being underutilized."
> — [00:12:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BlfmFCEycW0&t=754s)

**Context:** His foundational supply-side thesis for why a decentralized compute marketplace can exist; recurring figure across 2020-2021 appearances.

### [cloud-decentralization] Every layer of the cloud stack is decentralizing
> "Every part of the cloud stack is getting decentralized, and we put all of them together into one umbrella called d-cloud, and Akash we're positioning as the gateway for the d-cloud."
> — [00:17:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BlfmFCEycW0&t=1046s)

**Context:** Explaining the term "d-cloud" (which he says Akash coined): compute (Akash), storage (Sia/Filecoin), DNS (Handshake), key management (NuCypher).

### [local-compute] Plug in a GPU cluster and earn money
> "Anybody can go build a Supermini... it's just a GPU cluster. If you have a GPU cluster, when we have GPUs enabled on Akash you can plug in, earn money. Right now you can actually plug in any box, ideally sitting in a data center, to earn Akash token."
> — [00:35:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BlfmFCEycW0&t=2107s)

**Context:** Tamping down community hype about the experimental "Supermini" device while describing the model of individually-owned hardware earning AKT — an early statement of the home/edge-GPU-income idea.

### [gpu-economics] The invisible Amazon tax on machine learning
> "Today on the cloud, if you are a machine learning company using Amazon, 20% of your margins go to Amazon, and you and I, even though we don't run machine learning workloads, are actually paying an invisible Amazon tax."
> — [00:38:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BlfmFCEycW0&t=2282s)

**Context:** Making the case for a GPU marketplace on Akash; early (May 2021) version of his recurring "cloud tax" argument.

### [gpu-economics] Ethereum's merge will strand miner GPUs — Akash will absorb them
> "Ethereum network moving off proof of work and moving on to proof of stake implies that this entire GPU infrastructure Ethereum miners have built is going to become obsolete, and we want to tap into that... and enable a multi-tenancy GPU marketplace... I think it's going to be huge for us."
> — [00:38:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BlfmFCEycW0&t=2282s)

**Context:** Roadmap answer, over a year before the September 2022 merge; predicts stranded miner GPUs become Akash supply (quote spans into the [00:38:45] block).

### [cloud-decentralization] From 90-minute to one-minute deployments
> "Today it takes about 90 minutes for someone that has no idea what Akash [is] coming to the website and actually deploying an application... I want to bring that to one minute — click, click, click."
> — [00:38:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BlfmFCEycW0&t=2325s)

**Context:** His second core KPI (time-to-deploy) alongside active leases; achieving it is what he says product-market fit looks like.

### [cloud-decentralization] Nobody in d-web has product-market fit yet

**Context:** Unusually candid contrarian assessment of the 2021 decentralized-web sector, contrasting it with DeFi and NFTs which he says do have PMF. (Caption garbles "they're lying" as "they're like right.")

### [cloud-decentralization] Akash as the gateway to the decentralized web
