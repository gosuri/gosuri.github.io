## Summary

An Akash team explainer presented by Alani (not Greg) introducing fractional AKT (uAKT) for Testnet 3. It removes the implicit minimum deployment cost of one fractional AKT per block, which made extremely light workloads (a crypto wallet, a personal blog) more expensive than necessary and would worsen with token appreciation — a lightweight deployment could jump from $1 to $5/month if the token doubled or tripled. With uAKT, prices stay consistent and costs track actual resource consumption. Ends with the Testnet 3 signup/KYC workflow and the $200,000 prize pool.
