## Summary

Duplicate publication of "Conversations with Jack" S1E1 (also on Citizen Web3 as lCRG5sAp_Sc), recorded weeks before Akash Mainnet 2 (Feb 14, 2021). Host Jack Zampolin (early Akash investor/advisor) interviews Greg on his background (AngelHack, launching Firebase, Kubernetes contributions), why Akash left Ethereum prototyping after CryptoKitties congestion and went sovereign on Tendermint (Golang, shipping speed, deleting ~70,000 then ~100,000 lines of code after adopting Cosmos SDK), testnet results (3,800 deployments in two weeks, ~80 apps in Awesome Akash, ~480 monthly active developers vs. Polkadot's ~420, 130 Cosmos nodes run on Akash), the Thorchain bounty (~$4,000 in RUNE+AKT for a production-ready deployment guide, motivated by Thorchain's nodes concentrating on Amazon), the exclusive Equinix Metal launch partnership with bare-metal pricing 2-3x below market, and the IBC vision of composing Akash compute with decentralized storage (Filecoin/Arweave/Storj/Sia), NuCypher KMS, and Handshake DNS into a unified cloud that can replace centralized incumbents. Jack (not Greg) explains the Peggy Ethereum bridge/Uniswap listing plans and predicts a flood of smaller data centers joining post-launch.

## Predictions & Notable Claims

### [cloud-decentralization] Akash as the first open-source cloud
> "That gave way to really building a tool that can leverage Kubernetes and leverage the enormous amount of computing we have to build a second, new generation open source cloud — the first open source cloud — and that's really what Akash is."
> — [00:02:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=21oNz5O1qzk&t=126s)

**Context:** His founding framing: cloud infrastructure as the layer open source hadn't yet captured.

### [gpu-economics] Launch pricing 2-3x below market on bare metal
> "You're getting high quality, really high quality bare metal servers for next to nothing. I hope these prices will stay the way they are, but I think right off the bat we're looking for like two to three x lower than the market... this is bare metal, so there's nothing in the middle — you're just getting raw performance that is not possible on the cloud today."
> — [00:17:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=21oNz5O1qzk&t=1063s)

**Context:** On the exclusive Equinix Metal launch partnership for Mainnet 2; Equinix donated all testnet compute.

### [cloud-decentralization] Crypto nodes concentrated on Amazon — Thorchain bounty
> "They were having an effort to decentralize because a lot of their nodes are running on Amazon, and Thorchain — if you know, the team is an anonymous team — they embrace the decentralized values really well, to the core. And I reached out to them: look, we can run Thor nodes pretty well, how about doing a thing — and I gave a bounty... overall it's about four thousand dollars worth of RUNE tokens and AKT tokens just for writing a guide."
> — [00:13:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=21oNz5O1qzk&t=811s)

**Context:** Early version of his recurring "crypto runs on centralized cloud" critique; "the first time someone else is paying or incentivizing to put their nodes on Akash" [00:14:13].

### [cloud-decentralization] IBC removes the last obstacle to replacing centralized cloud
> "You have to use different tokens and different signatures and all kinds of things — so imagine trying to automate a solution that spans across these networks, like impossible, right? IBC is going to solve that in an amazing way... the real power is when you're going to solve these friction problems for decentralized cloud adoption. Once you solve that, there's nothing in front of us to actually replace incumbent, like centralized cloud providers, in a unified way."
> — [00:22:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=21oNz5O1qzk&t=1359s)

**Context:** The "interoperable cloud" vision: Akash compute + Filecoin/Arweave/Sia storage + NuCypher KMS + Handshake DNS composed via IBC; quote spans into the [00:23:21] block.

### [other] Testnet traction: more active developers than Polkadot
> "When we launched our latest testnet we had 3,800 or so deployments in two weeks... about 80 applications that people shared... and about 480 or so monthly active developers right now on the testnet, which is phenomenal — for comparison, Polkadot has about 420 monthly active developers."
> — [00:09:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=21oNz5O1qzk&t=596s)

**Context:** Pre-Mainnet-2 metrics (quote spans into the [00:10:40] block); the 2019 incentivized testnet had ~1,900 deployments and 130 providers [00:07:50].

### [crypto-depin] Pay for compute with any token — the end state
> "Ideally you want to be able to pay for compute — staking is one thing, but actually paying for compute using Ethereum or an ERC-20 token, that means any stable currency on ERC, is the ideal scenario that we are all going towards... we're going to do this in multiple stages: first we're going to do a wrapped bridge that will eventually become a lot [more] native."
> — [00:29:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=21oNz5O1qzk&t=1784s)

**Context:** Closing answer following Jack's Peggy bridge/Uniswap explanation; multi-token payment later became a shipped Akash feature.
