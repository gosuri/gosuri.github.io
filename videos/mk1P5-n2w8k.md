## Summary

Weekly Osmosis community Twitter space (hosts Kevin Barry and "dynamic manic," with co-founder Sunny Aggarwal) featuring Greg as sole guest. Sunny opens with Osmosis updates ($1B liquidity, $120M daily volume ATHs, superfluid staking, bech32 IBC routing, Gravity Bridge, stableswap plans) and quips that 2022 is already "the year of app chains." Greg then explains Akash as a marketplace for compute cycles with reverse-auction pricing at one-third to one-fifth of Amazon's price, permissionless and censorship-resistant, and contrasts it with Golem/iExec (no long-running jobs) and Flux (permissioned, "vampire attack" copycat). Much of the conversation is developer-focused: the SDL stack definition language gaining variables and logic, Terraform and GitHub Actions integrations, a HashiCorp-inspired CLI, and the Firebase/Angular and Kubernetes analogies for why builder-first framework integrations drive exponential adoption. Roadmap items: February testnet bringing SSH access and persistent storage (VPS-style capability ~45 days out), a decentralized services marketplace by end of 2022 mirroring Amazon's ~300 managed services, and a coming GPU market. Sunny pitches DAO-controlled front ends paid from community pools (Greg: "data services for DAOs, that's the future") and notes post-Merge Ethereum GPUs becoming idle supply. In Q&A Greg describes the ~79 providers (Equinix as biggest supplier, ~85% of data-center capacity unused), Akash as a global spot market, home gaming/mining computers soon providing GPU compute, Texas renewable-energy overage powering profitable data centers, provider-level content moderation, community-driven "client acquisition" via mining communities and DAOs, and why Cosmos assets (including AKT) aren't yet on Binance/Coinbase — predicting one "slew" of Cosmos listings.
