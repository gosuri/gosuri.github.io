## Summary

The inaugural Akash Developer Relations Office Hours livestream, hosted by community lead Adam Wozny and devrel head Eric Zitlow — Greg Osuri does not appear. Community updates cover Lisbon "crypto month" (Solana Breakpoint, HackAtom), the Akash Insiders program (28 insiders worldwide, an in-person summit planned), and upcoming monthly office hours plus a community demo day. Technical/challenge updates: the Arweave/ArDrive "permaweb" hackathon (file system, NFT, and bootstrapping challenges), a Polygon migration challenge matching up to 100 AKT (~$250 then) per project migrated off AWS onto Akash, and the HackAtom Cosmos Omnibus challenge ($35k/$15k prizes) to add one-click deployable Cosmos chain nodes. Roadmap items: persistent storage (direct-attached-style volumes so databases/stateful workloads survive reboots — explicitly augmenting rather than replacing Filecoin/Arweave/Skynet-style storage), persistent logging for post-mortem debugging, Terraform/Ansible scripts to stand up providers easily (including home-server providers "in your garage"), a CLI rework targeted around January, and Akash.js library work (shout-outs to community contributor Space Potato). The grants program is described as community-funded through the Community Awards Board, ranging from $100 to $100,000.
