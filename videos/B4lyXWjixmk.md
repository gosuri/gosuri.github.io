## Summary

A community tutorial in which the Praetor App team demos setting up an Akash GPU provider: connecting a wallet, choosing single-server (k3s) vs. multi-server (k8s) setup, installing Nvidia toolkit/drivers, importing the provider wallet, pointing DNS records, and setting pricing — noting Praetor had just moved price scripting from AKT to USD (e.g., $150 per GPU per month). The demo ends with the provider live and its T4 GPU biddable. Speakers are Praetor developers, not Greg Osuri.
