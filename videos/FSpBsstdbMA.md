## Summary

A clip (from a community discussion, in the wake of 2022's bridge hacks) explaining why Cosmos IBC is fundamentally more secure than bridges: relayers only pass packets — assets stay secured by the sending/receiving chains' stake, so "once the relayer has relayed, the relayer can die and the assets are still secure." The speaker (consistent with Greg's voice in companion Akash Analysis clips, though the clip has no intro) contrasts a bridge blowing up with you on it versus a Star Wars-style transporter. He then flags the big attack vector for Cosmos chains: if a chain's TVL exceeds its market cap, an attacker could theoretically buy majority voting power and drain it — "the security really depends... only as secure as its market cap" and how many tokens are bonded. He concludes Akash has a safer security profile because it has no third-party smart-contract execution risk and secures no assets on-chain: "Akash really at the end of the day is just a coordination protocol."

**Context:** Names the main future attack vector for Cosmos-based systems; adds "we're yet to see a complete proof of stake blockchain takeover." **Speaker:** uncredited clip; likely Greg.
