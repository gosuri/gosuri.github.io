## Summary

A clip from a founders' discussion on Akash's origins and direction. Co-founder Adam Bozanich answers first: the internet "had kind of lost its way" — designed as decentralized nodes that could withstand nuclear blasts, it devolved to where "if us-west-1 goes down on AWS, no apps work anymore" — and deployment choices were either easy-but-expensive or cheap-but-painful; Akash's marketplace unlocks the huge untapped supply of compute. Greg then focuses on Akash Console: web3 lacks "traditional engagement infrastructure," so Console instruments the user journey to get statistically significant data on friction points, enabling aggressive developer marketing (click-to-deploy from blog posts) and "much much higher user activity." Notably, he predicts the bear market "is going to be a lot worse... a prolonged market," and frames it as time to hunker down and hyper-focus on users until "the light finally comes."
