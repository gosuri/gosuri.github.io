## Summary

A solo Greg Osuri keynote at Permissionless (Blockworks PMLS 2025) laying out his fullest "superintelligence needs the supercloud" thesis. He opens with Kardashev Type 1 framing (500x today's energy) and casts AI as "the next fire." The bottleneck argument: US data centers used 176 TWh in 2023 (~4.4% of national electricity), DOE warns of up to 12% by 2028; Gartner says 40% of AI data centers may be unable to secure power by 2026; Virginia/Texas utilities are at capacity and California interconnect queues run 3-5 years — "the bottleneck is electricity," not GPUs. His three levers: radical software efficiency (30-50% inference savings from optimization/scheduling), massive clean supply (renewables, geothermal, advanced nuclear — grid integration being the real blocker), and decentralized compute: 10,000 smaller sites instead of 10 mega data centers, down to "your basement rig." He cites 2025 decentralized-training breakthroughs (Pluralis async swarm training, Gensyn's 72B model on a decentralized testnet, Bagel's ZK-LoRA verification, Nous's 405B open model) as proof the "planetary AI mesh" is real, declaring "the future isn't hyperscale, it's hyperdistributed."

He then unveils the Akash Star Cluster roadmap — Phase 1 data-center "star nodes" run by node keepers, Phase 2 telco edge (fiber exchanges, cell sites), Phase 3 homes, schools, and communities where "every extra GPU becomes part of a global AI mesh" and every participant earns — funded by Starbonds, NFTs representing ownership in specific racks paying pro-rata on-chain revenue. Energy-aware compute follows: jobs chasing solar peaks in Arizona and Kansas wind, "power attracts compute." He closes with a national-security/geopolitical case for distribution as "the American advantage" and five policy asks (rooftop-solar-style permitting for small data centers, interconnect reform, tax incentives, SMR licensing, DePIN legal clarity).

## Predictions & Notable Claims

### [energy] AI could eat 12% of the US grid by 2028 — the bottleneck is electricity
> "In 2023, US data centers consumed 176 terawatt hours — about 4.4% of the entire national electricity output. And the Department of Energy now warns that by 2028 that could be more than triple, to as much as 12% of the grid... AI isn't just a user of compute, it's a devourer of energy."
> — [00:01:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FBHsD1bgtvc&t=88s)

**Context:** Opening problem statement; he adds Gartner's forecast that by 2026, 40% of AI data centers may be unable to secure sufficient power: "This is a bottleneck. Not bandwidth, not GPUs — it's electricity."

### [local-compute] 10,000 small sites instead of 10 mega data centers — down to your basement rig
> "Decentralized compute — this is a game changer. Instead of building 10 massive data centers, what if we could tap into 10,000 smaller ones? Colos, edge nodes, university clusters, even your basement rig. And that's what Akash does: ties them all together, monetizes their capacity, and routes workloads wherever they can run efficiently."
> — [00:05:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FBHsD1bgtvc&t=318s)

**Context:** Third of his three levers to break the energy bottleneck, after radical efficiency and clean supply; "we're not waiting for a new hyperscale zone to get approved — we're using what we already have."

### [decentralized-ai] 2025 broke the "decentralized AI is impossible" assumption
> "Until recently, decentralized AI was just considered impossible — too slow, too fragmented. But in 2025, that changed. Pluralis introduced async swarm training... Gensyn trained a 72 billion parameter model on a decentralized testnet — no campus, no cloud, just thousands of volunteers with GPUs. Bagel used ZK-LoRA... to verify decentralized fine-tuning without needing to rerun it. Nous is training a 405B parameter open model using distributed GPUs."
> — [00:06:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FBHsD1bgtvc&t=405s)

**Context:** Evidence roll for "models trained across continents with compute contributed from living rooms, research labs and micro data centers."

### [cloud-decentralization] The future isn't hyperscale — it's hyperdistributed
> "This is the architecture of the future: a planetary AI mesh, alive with local energy and local compute. Redundant by design because there's no single points of failure. Open to anyone, anywhere... The future isn't hyperscale, it's hyperdistributed."
> — [00:08:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FBHsD1bgtvc&t=492s)

**Context:** The keynote's thesis line, tying decentralized training breakthroughs to "intelligence more resilient, more inclusive, and more aligned with democratic values."

### [local-compute] Phase 3: homes, schools, communities — every GPU earns in the new compute economy
> "Finally, the long tail: personal rigs, campus clusters, solar powered school servers. Every extra GPU becomes a part of a global AI mesh. Every participant earns by contributing compute. This isn't charity — this is the new compute economy."
> — [00:10:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FBHsD1bgtvc&t=631s)

**Context:** The Akash Star Cluster roadmap: Phase 1 data-center star nodes, Phase 2 telco edge nodes, Phase 3 homes/schools; "not just scale, but sovereignty... a planetary supercloud, node by node, powered by the people."

### [gpu-economics] A gigawatt of AI compute doesn't need one building
> "One Nvidia GB200 NVL72 rack draws about 120 kW... To reach a gigawatt of compute, about 8,300 of these racks — traditionally that would mean a dozen hyperscale campuses. But with Akash, those racks can be anywhere... We're not bottlenecked by real estate or substation permits. We route around the constraints... Gigawatt power without a gigawatt footprint."
> — [00:11:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FBHsD1bgtvc&t=680s)

**Context:** The disaggregation math behind the Star Cluster; spreading infrastructure unlocks cheap energy, localized cooling, and idle-hardware reactivation.

### [crypto-depin] Starbonds: NFT rack ownership paying on-chain AI revenue
> "Each Starbond is an NFT that represents ownership in a specific node. When you mint one, you're backing the deployment of a real-world rack by a node keeper in a real facility serving real AI workloads. In return, you earn a pro-rata share of the node's revenue automatically on chain without intermediaries. It's like staking, but instead of securing a blockchain, you're powering intelligence."
> — [00:12:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FBHsD1bgtvc&t=769s)

**Context:** His funding model replacing "mega project loans" — "a new asset class: performance-yielding AI infrastructure. No speculation, no hype — just hardware, revenue and compute."

### [energy] Flip the model: power attracts compute
> "When you decentralize compute, it becomes mobile. It can move with energy. Imagine AI training jobs chasing solar peaks across Arizona rooftops, inference pipelines migrating dynamically with Kansas wind, GPUs spinning up where renewables are cheap and idling when they're not. Instead of compute demanding power, power attracts compute. This flips the entire model."
> — [00:14:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FBHsD1bgtvc&t=855s)

**Context:** "Energy-aware AI" — programmable workloads responding in real time to price, carbon intensity, and load; "we turn the grid from a constraint into a canvas."

### [decentralized-ai] Decentralization is a national-security advantage
> "When compute lives in a dozen hyperscale zones, it's easy to surveil, easy to disrupt, easy to control. But when compute lives in 12,000 locations it's resilient, it's redundant, and it's harder to censor, harder to kill, and harder to co-opt... We don't win the AI age by concentrating compute. We win by distributing it. And that's the American advantage."
> — [00:15:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FBHsD1bgtvc&t=943s)

**Context:** The geopolitical close — "defense by distribution, security by architecture" — followed by five policy asks including rooftop-solar-style permitting for small data centers and SMR licensing.

### [energy] A Type 1 civilization emerges from a network of millions, not one cloud provider
> "A type one civilization doesn't emerge from one cloud provider. It emerges from a network of millions — coordinated, open, and alive... Let's build a cloud that reflects our values. A cloud that can't be shut down. A cloud we own together. Let's meet at Kardashev 1."
> — [00:19:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FBHsD1bgtvc&t=1164s)

**Context:** Closing callback to the Kardashev opening (500x today's energy for Type 1, "AI is the next fire").
