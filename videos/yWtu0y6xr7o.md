## Summary

Akash Weekly Twitter Spaces hosted by Nadia Bajuelo, recorded days after the FTX collapse ("these are some of those weeks we look back and be like... we survived"). Greg's updates: Mainnet 4 is expected around November 17, 2022, headlined by the IP address marketplace — a step toward cloud parity that unblocks Solana node hosting (timely, as Hetzner had just purged crypto workloads, taking down ~20% of Solana's network and prompting Anatoly Yakovenko to endorse Akash) plus privacy projects like Mask and Alter. Mainnet 5 with GPU support is said to be coming soon after.

Guests Lex and Errol Avellino of Passage (Cosmos metaverse/events platform, Strange Clan NFTs) discuss their roadmap, cloud-streamed Unreal Engine worlds, and using Akash for websites today with plans for Akash GPU-powered game streaming. The conversation turns to generative AI: Greg argues compute demand for generative AI is "doubling every four months," GPUs are scarce because owners won't sell, so the answer is tapping idle deployed GPUs — even floating the idea of a DAO funding A100 data centers that amortize in six to eight months. In the AMA, Greg outlines the stable-payment "take-and-burn" design (non-AKT stablecoin payments taxed, initially ~20%, with proceeds used to buy and burn AKT), notes a Stable Diffusion training run cost ~$600K, and answers a bear-market question: Overclock Labs has runway for the winter, unannounced Fortune-50/enterprise partnerships are in progress, and he predicts enterprise adoption and generative AI's GPU hunger (plus ~$19B of GPUs idled by the Ethereum merge) will be catalysts for the next bull run.
