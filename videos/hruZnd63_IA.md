## Summary

Second 2015 Overclock product demo narrated by Greg (pre-Akash Network era). He deploys a Go web app via `git push` using standard Heroku buildpacks ("if your app works on Heroku it should work here as well"), demonstrates zero-downtime rolling updates by hitting a version endpoint every 100 ms during a deploy, and shows atomic deploys: a build with an intentional panic fails health checks and is never promoted, leaving users unaffected. Docker image deploys are supported as an alternative to buildpacks. Pure product demo of Overclock's PaaS tooling; no predictions or market commentary.
