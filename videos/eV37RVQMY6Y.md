## Summary

An Akash Network marketing/explainer video (narrator uncredited, not identified as Greg) promoting MiniMax 2.5 on AkashML for AI agent workloads. Argument: people running always-on agents (e.g., OpenClaw) waste ~80% of AI spend routing routine tasks — tool calling, structured outputs, code generation, "70 to 80% of your pipeline" — to frontier models ("like hiring a Formula 1 driver for your Uber ride"), with power users spending $500-$2,000+/month, inflated further by reasoning models' thinking tokens (3-5x visible output). MiniMax 2.5 is claimed to benchmark within a percentage point of Claude Opus 4.6 at roughly 1/12th the token cost ($0.30/M input on AkashML vs. $5/M for Opus-level models). Prescription: model tiering — frontier for deep reasoning, MiniMax for the bulk, a lightweight model like Qwen for simple calls.
