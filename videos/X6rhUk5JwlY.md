## Summary

A panel at Akash's own Accelerate 2025 event, but Greg Osuri does not appear on stage. Tommy Eastman (Plain Text Capital) moderates Haseeb Qureshi (Dragonfly), Abhay Kumar (Helium protocol lead), and Mahesh (Escape Velocity) debating "Is DePIN real?" Haseeb argues on-chain DePIN revenue is "a rounding error" (~$3-10B market cap vs. $3T crypto), that DePINs may be self-sustaining but never hundred-billion-dollar networks, that token economics "corrupts the price signal" from both supply and demand sides, and that AI-data DePINs face intrinsic fraud problems (synthetic data passing AI checks, model collapse). Abhay shares Helium's lessons — removing crypto from the subscriber experience activated growth, bring-your-own-device on supply, "we went decentralized way too early on governance" — and wishes for a DePIN around real-time reagent/genomic synthesis for biosciences. Mahesh sees most DePINs becoming good $25-50M "mom and pop" networks, highlights crypto-native financing (Daylight's $85M solar credit facility) and GeoNet-style stablecoin-incentivized long-tail data plays for robotics. Relevant to the archive only as context for Akash's event programming; no Osuri statements to extract.
