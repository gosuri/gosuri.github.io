## Summary

Akash Accelerate 2025 talk by Nirmal Krishnan, co-founder of Chakra Labs, on building an "open data protocol for AI": LLM providers trap consumer-generated data in silos (Grok/Twitter, OpenAI/Reddit, Gemini/YouTube), so Chakra bridges AI developers with ethically sourced data from incentivized consumers via a low-cost data warehouse (running on bare-metal compute like Akash), an open data marketplace, and "subnet" data-collection businesses — Scout (300K downloads, claimed largest robots.txt-compliant Twitter/LinkedIn corpus) and the upcoming Pango (paying consumers in USDC for computer-use recordings for post-training). Greg does not appear in this video.
