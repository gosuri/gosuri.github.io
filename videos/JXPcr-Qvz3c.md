## Summary

Host Jason Rigden interviews Nick Alesandro of Akash Network (pre-token-sale, testnet era, August 2018). Nick lays out the early Akash pitch: data is doubling yearly while cloud capacity is not; Google, Amazon, and Microsoft form an oligopoly; ~85% of privately owned server capacity sits idle; Akash liberates it via an on-chain reverse-auction marketplace for Kubernetes-orchestrated Docker containers, with SGX trusted execution, provider staking/reputation, and a "decentralized infrastructure is a moral imperative" framing. Greg Osuri does not appear; he is mentioned twice — as a co-founder with deep Kubernetes/Go background, and as having just ported his personal blog to run on the Akash testnet the night before recording. No direct quotes of Greg, so nothing is extracted per third-party rules. Historically interesting as a snapshot of Akash's 2018 architecture (collateral-backed leases, planned Spark/ML workload support, "cheaper, faster, better" positioning).
