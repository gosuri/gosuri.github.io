## Summary

An Akash team explainer presented by Alani (not Greg) announcing the partnership with Chia Network (30+ exabytes of contributed disk space) to decentralize storage on web3. Akash became "the world's first d-web platform to support plot creation," offering on-demand hardware for Chia plotting (with a `plotting=true` provider attribute for machines that can create plots in under an hour), persistent-storage-based farming planned for Q1 2022, and a pitch for Chia plotters to repurpose their expensive plotting hardware as Akash providers.
