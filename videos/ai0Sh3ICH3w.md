## Summary

A #shorts clip from the Web3 Working Group podcast with one of Greg's clearest statements of his home-compute thesis: beyond data centers, homes are full of idle compute — the PlayStation and Xbox behind him with powerful GPUs used "once or twice a year," M1 Macs with powerful GPUs idle overnight while still drawing power and cooling. Globally spread underutilized resources waste enormous energy, "and that needs to be fixed." His conclusion: "web3 is greener for me — web3 is about efficiency and improving the global energy usage efficiency."

## Topics

local compute, home gpus, underutilization, energy efficiency, web3, green computing, consumer devices

## Predictions & Notable Claims

### [local-compute] Idle home devices — consoles and M1 Macs — are untapped compute
> "Just look at the compute that we have unused in our homes. I have a PlayStation and Xbox right behind me that's just sitting there — I probably use it once or twice a year — but just sitting there with powerful GPUs. And the computers — your Macs have M1 chips, which are very powerful GPUs, that are not used in the night... but you're paying cooling for it. So there's a lot of underutilized resources that are spread globally that is using enormous amounts of energy that needs to be fixed."

**Context:** His signature local/home-compute supply argument, extended from data centers to consumer devices.
