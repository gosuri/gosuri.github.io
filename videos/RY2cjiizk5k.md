## Summary

Akash Network livestream hosted by community lead Adam Wozney with Andrew Mello (head of mining at Overclock Labs) and John Michael Hands (VP of storage at Chia Network). Greg Osuri does not appear. The session covers Mainnet 3 features (fractional uAKT, persistent storage, container limits raised to 256 CPUs / 512GB RAM / 32TB storage) and how they enable in-memory BladeBit Chia plot creation on Akash — cutting plot time from ~60 minutes to under 10 (fastest ~4 minutes at 110 threads). Hands explains Chia's proof-of-space-and-time consensus (~200,000 nodes, claimed more decentralized than Bitcoin, ~1000x lower energy), farming economics (~90% of TCO is the hard drive), and plotting-as-a-service pricing (~$0.20/plot, ~$2/TB). Mello demos deployment via Akashlytics, pitches idle Chia plotting rigs becoming Akash providers ($120-800/month per workload), and discusses future farming on Akash persistent storage. Historical snapshot of Akash's pre-GPU, mining-adjacent use-case era.
