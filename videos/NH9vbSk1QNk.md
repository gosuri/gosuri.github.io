## Summary

A Coinage (Zack Guzman) news explainer on the 2023 GPU shortage — Elon Musk's "harder to get than drugs" line, Nvidia joining the trillion-dollar club, ChatGPT's ~$700K/day compute costs, and the big three clouds controlling two-thirds of the market — and how crypto projects (Akash, Render, Arweave, Filecoin) aim to fix it with Airbnb-style marketplaces for idle chips. Greg appears in brief interview soundbites arguing GPUs are the lifeblood of AI, that Akash is the Airbnb-like layer for cloud capacity, and that the Ethereum merge unleashed ~$19B of idle GPUs. Render founder Jules Urbach is also quoted saying decentralized GPU scaling is inevitable. The piece notes AKT up 250%+ YTD and Render 5x, while cautioning these networks remain tiny vs AWS's $20B+ quarterly revenue.
