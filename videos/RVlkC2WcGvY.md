## Summary

Weekly Akash community Twitter Space (simulcast to YouTube) hosted by community events manager Nadia Bajuelo, with Greg Osuri giving the August 2022 recap and an AMA, plus an introduction of new developer relations lead Ali Merchant. Greg opens with Hetzner's announcement banning crypto workloads — notable because Hetzner hosted much of the Cosmos ecosystem's infrastructure and a large share of Ethereum nodes. He frames it as validation of Akash's reason to exist ("censorship-resistant, sovereign, always-on cloud") and predicts a wave of similar bans from centralized providers, eventually including the biggest clouds. He walks through ecosystem/product updates: incremental protocol features (persistent storage, static IP leases, upcoming private registries, private networking, GPUs), protocol clients (Spheron, Cloudmos SDL Builder, Praetor containerization, Vultr/vexilla fiat on-ramp work, thirdweb demo), a community-built Terraform provider that drew interest from HashiCorp, and Chia choosing Akash as launch partner for its bladebit miner. He revisits the 2018 whitepaper thesis that cloud gets expensive long-term, citing Walmart repatriating data centers, and argues underutilized enterprise capacity will flow onto Akash. He also comments on the Tornado Cash developer arrest as an attack on free software. The AMA covers validator decentralization (many Akash validators ironically ran on Hetzner; why validators shouldn't run on Akash itself), enterprise BD strategy, and upcoming support for new container runtimes like Firecracker. Closes with hiring updates (senior engineers, VP of marketing).
