## Summary

Akash Weekly Twitter Spaces hosted by Nadia Bajuelo, with Greg Osuri delivering updates before guests Kate Kim and Harshita Mathur of Mantra discuss their validator business and Akash partnership. Greg opens with Mainnet 4 launching the next day (Prop 27 passed, 50+ providers ready), unlocking IP-address support for workloads like Solana, Handshake, and Urbit nodes. Recorded days after the FTX collapse, Greg frames the contagion (tracing back to Luna) as the strongest proof point yet that "every centralized player that we trust... failed us and only thing that hasn't failed is decentralization."

He previews "Economics 2.0": a proposed 20% take rate on non-AKT (stablecoin/USDC) settlements, used to market-buy and burn AKT, so take income returns "in a different form." He argues AI/ML is Akash's biggest coming demand driver — GPU need "doubling every four months" — with ~$19B of GPUs unlocked from Ethereum mining post-Merge, and teases NDA'd enterprise distribution deals within 6-12 months.

In the AMA, Greg addresses the LBRY ruling and SEC risk (arguing sufficiently decentralized networks whose value accrues from many non-colluding entities are commodities, not securities), advises new projects to find product-market fit before launching a token (predicting VC investment "dries up" around September 2023), and closes with his recurring thesis: cloud is the fabric of modern society, three companies control 60-70% of compute, and "the cloud needs to be a public utility — the only data structure to enable a public utility is a blockchain." Dated markers: Mainnet 4 eve, FTX collapse aftermath, Hetzner banning PoS validators.
