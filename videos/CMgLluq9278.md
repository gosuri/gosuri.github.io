## Summary

First video of a community series on the Akash GPU testnet: a step-by-step tutorial for creating a single-server GPU provider using Praetor. Covers hardware requirements (8 CPU cores, 16 GB RAM, 140 GB storage, Nvidia GPU, ~40 minutes total), creating a Keplr wallet, fixing sudo permissions over SSH, importing the provider wallet, setting pricing, and adding DNS A records. Ends with the testnet provider live and immediately receiving a GPU bid request. Narrated by a community member, not Greg Osuri.
