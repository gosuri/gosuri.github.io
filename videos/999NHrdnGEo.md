## Summary

An AtomDenver panel on AI and crypto with Greg Osuri (Akash), Lex (Passage, virtual events), and Harry (Soarchain, vehicle DePIN), moderated by a Secret Network host. Greg introduces Akash as "the world's first open supercloud" — a cloud that decouples the control plane from the resource plane to tap globally distributed, locked-away capacity, sometimes 10x cheaper than Amazon. He quips that "GPUs is the new spice" and Akash are "the spice dealers." Asked to define AI, Greg (who notes formal AI training from 20 years ago) calls it "just a better paintbrush" — a recollection, automation, and emotional engine. On AI doom, he places Akash firmly in the acceleration camp, saying it's far too early to fear models ("ChatGPT forgot how to code"), while joking that Akash Network translated to English is literally "Skynet" — an unstoppable network agents could use to self-train and self-replicate. On AI-crypto synergy he cites a Semafor article arguing "AI needs crypto more than crypto needs AI": crypto excels at resource optimization and can unlock idle GPUs (from miners and ML companies between training runs) permissionlessly, and blockchain provenance will matter because synthetic content will soon be indistinguishable from real. He closes recommending Morpheus (Erik Voorhees's decentralized chat-model aggregator) and Nous Research, both building on Akash.
