## Summary

An Akash Network animated explainer (narrator uncredited, not identified as Greg) of Burn-Mint Equilibrium (BME). Worked example: Bob pays $100 by credit card; the backend buys $100 of AKT off-market and burns it to mint $100 of ACT credits, which Bob spends on a provider's GPU server without AKT-volatility risk. Providers later burn ACT to mint AKT at the current market price. If AKT rises during the job, fewer AKT are minted than burned (net supply reduction); if it falls, more must be minted, so a monitored collateral ratio and circuit breakers throttle ACT minting to prevent runaway inflation while ensuring providers get paid. Framing: BME "links real compute usage directly with AKT's token economy."
