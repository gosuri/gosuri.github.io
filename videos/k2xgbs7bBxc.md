## Summary

Part two of the "Akashonomics" community series, hosted by Akash community lead Adam Wozny with community member Cheng ("changestan", a former traditional-finance risk professional), joined briefly by Overclock Labs CTO Adam Bozanich. Greg Osuri is mentioned only in passing ("when Akash was just a twinkle in Greg and Adam's eye") and does not speak. The call explains how Akash escrow accounts enable trustless, time-based lease settlement between tenants and providers without block-by-block micropayments (minimum 5 AKT funding, provider withdrawal mechanism), and how Cosmos SDK inflation works: bonded ratio vs. the ~67% target, min/max guardrails, and the then-recent Chandra Station governance proposal that brought inflation from the mid-30s down to ~24% toward the whitepaper's 388M max supply. Cheng argues higher early inflation rewarded early adopters who took on risk, and sketches scenarios where inflation could rise again (competing DeFi yield pulling AKT out of staking, or heavy network usage lowering the bonded ratio). Adam Bozanich confirms the planned inflation decay curve is an exponential decay recomputed block by block with governance-configurable parameters, and previews a new authorized-spend mechanism: the faucet will grant an on-chain authorization to spend its AKT on deployments rather than transferring tokens (which people were liquidating). Network stats cited: 70,000+ all-time workloads, ~700 daily active. Ends with a plug for ETHDenver 2022.
