## Summary

Hosts Mahesh and Connor Lovely (Proof of Coverage, a DePIN podcast) interview Greg two weeks after Akash's Mainnet 6 GPU launch (August 31, 2023). After an opening tangent on the Mexican Congress "aliens" hearing and Indian mythology, Greg retells learning to code in 1990s India without a computer, AngelHack, Kubernetes/Terraform contributions, and the supercloud concept — a decentralized control plane federating public/private clouds, to which Akash added a marketplace. He explains why NVIDIA's deliberate market fragmentation (prioritizing CoreWeave-class boutique clouds over hyperscalers; CoreWeave holding ~35,000 H100s, "way more than Google Cloud") plus startups locked into year-long prepaid GPU contracts they can't fully use creates Akash's opening: "Airbnb for high-end GPUs," a sub-leasing secondary market. Launch traction: ~170 high-end GPUs including H100s/A100s at 100% utilization (lower-end chips ~50%), Foundry (DCG) showing up as a provider unannounced, AkashML onboarding 40 applications in week one with Greg personally running concierge calls, and friction converting non-crypto AI developers ("$10,000 in your MetaMask... is actually very scary"). The closing segment is his crypto×AI worldview: crypto is phenomenal at sourcing supply, AI at sourcing demand; the race to AGI must not be won by a single corporation; decentralized data marketplaces, incentivized evals (BitTensor), provenance for deepfakes; and chips as the choke point of AI power. Ends teasing $1.89/hr H100/A100 pricing on AkashML.
