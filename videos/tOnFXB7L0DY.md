## Summary

A "Staking Mondays" live interview with Staking Rewards co-founder Mirko, with community Q&A and an AKT giveaway. Greg walks through the Akash origin story: building cloud at Kaiser Permanente in 2008, founding AngelHack, watching AWS abandon its developer-first ethos, and the 2013 insight that no platform was both easy to use and easy to scale — plus opaque cloud pricing and ~85% of data-center compute sitting unused. He explains the pivot from an edge-computing product (BitTorrent-style replication with Merkle-tree integrity checks that "basically built blockchain" before adopting one), the failed Ethereum prototype (CryptoKitties crashed it), and the organic move to Tendermint/Cosmos SDK, where Adam Bozanich deleted 150k lines of custom code. He lays out how Akash wins against AWS/Google: capability-matching Amazon's ~300 managed services via a decentralized services marketplace (targeted end of 2022, designed with bash creator Brian Fox) where open-source creators like Percona or Redis Labs run managed offerings and earn royalties. On tokenomics, he defends the ~30% inflation / 40-45% staking APR as "bootstrapping free markets by borrowing from the future" — a decay-curve distribution to early believers — and previews "economics 2.0": take income for stakers, a deflationary AKT, and a Terra-UST-style ecosystem stablecoin for payment stability. The most prediction-dense section is his moon scenario: at full success everyone uses Akash without knowing it — Teslas, fridges, gaming consoles and phones earning income from idle compute, an ARM/GPU build for phones "this quarter and next," GPUs arriving in three to five months, Ethereum miners repurposing hardware to Akash post-merge, and the Super Mini returning once GPUs land. Roadmap items: persistent storage (February testnet), IP address marketplace, log retention, and powering Solana RPC infrastructure.
