## Summary

A short testimonial Greg recorded for Solana's channel in April 2020. He introduces himself as "an applied economist, computer scientist and a visual storyteller" and Akash as a peer-to-peer marketplace for cloud computing resources. He praises Solana's proof of history for improving the trustability of the time source, reducing messages needed for finality and scaling better than sharding approaches ("by solving for time you can reduce the amount of verifications required, thereby increasing the throughput of a distributed system"). He recounts meeting Anatoly Yakovenko in late 2017 and being impressed by the team's Qualcomm operating-system background — crediting them with the Brew OS "used by probably 50% of the mobile phones before the iPhones and Androids were around" — making them the team he'd trust "to solve the time problem in a decentralized manner."
