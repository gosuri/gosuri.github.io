## Summary

Akash office hours hosted by community lead Adam Wozny with Eric Zietlow (Akash director of developer relations) and Mike Cullinan (core contributor at Archway, an incentivized CosmWasm smart-contract chain for Cosmos). Greg Osuri is not on this stream. Topics: Akash's January 2022 product update — deployments roughly doubled/tripled in a month, driven largely by the Chia plotting community, with the network routinely hitting max capacity; the upcoming incentivized Testnet 3; Archway's model of redirecting protocol rewards (gas fees, inflation share, contract premiums) to dapp developers; and collaboration ideas, notably running validator nodes and dapp front/back ends on Akash to escape AWS concentration (Eric cites roughly two-thirds of Ethereum infrastructure on AWS; Mike cites 50-60% of well-known networks on AWS). Eric also mentions GPU support for AI/ML and pixel streaming as a roadmap item "coming in the near future," notes no plans for stablecoin payments at that time, and pitches ETHDenver 2022 attendance. Since Greg does not speak, no predictions are attributed.
