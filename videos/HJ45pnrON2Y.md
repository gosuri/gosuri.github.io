## Summary

Talk at Akash Accelerate 2025 by Zack Abrams, journalist at The Block, on why the open-source AI image/video ecosystem (ComfyUI, HiDream, Wan/VACE, SAM 2, LoRAs, ControlNets, regional prompting) unlocks far more creative control than closed front-ends like Google Imagen or OpenAI's image model, which he calls "high-quality slot machines." He demos multi-stage denoising workflows, video inpainting (replacing a dragon with a cat), and character swaps, and notes these models are now good enough to run on consumer hardware, including his MacBook Pro. Greg only receives a thank-you; he does not speak in this video.
