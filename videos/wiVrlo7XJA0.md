## Summary

Official livestream of Akash Accelerate 2025 in New York (1,800 registrations for 500 seats, ~1,300 watching online). Greg opens the day, then the content runs: a "Beyond Central Models" VC panel (Chad Fowler, Casey Caruso/Topology, Jake Brukhman/CoinFund, Ben Parr) on decentralized AI's cost advantages and Pluralis's lossless gradient-compression milestone; a Daylight Energy talk (Jason Badeaux) on the grid's D+ report card, 5-10 year interconnection queues, and decentralized energy; Greg's fireside with Javier Villamizar (SoftBank operating partner on the $500B Stargate program) covering the $1T Arizona/Illinois robotics hub, hybrid energy, chip efficiency, and rapid-fire 2030 predictions; showcases from Passage, an AI-video creator, Chakra, Vertical AI, Levangie Labs, and NEAR's Illia Polosukhin on decentralized confidential ML; a spicy "is DePIN real" debate (Haseeb Qureshi, Abhay Kumar/Helium, Mahesh); an "Investing in Intelligence" panel; and a closing Nous Research talk ("Skynet was a closed source company"). The centerpiece is Greg's ~45-minute keynote: AI's energy demand makes watts "the new silicon"; three levers (radical efficiency, clean supply, decentralized compute) fix it; decentralized training is already real (Pluralis swarm training, Gensyn's 72B on-chain model, Nous's 405B run, Bagel's ZK-LoRA); and Akash's answer is the Star Cluster roadmap — data-center star nodes in 2025, telco edge in 2026, homes and schools in 2027 — funded by Starbonds, fractional on-chain ownership of GB200 racks. He frames it civilizationally (Kardashev Type One, "let's build a cloud no one can shut down") and closes with five policy asks (permitting, interconnect reform, tax credits, SMRs, DePIN legal clarity). Quotes below are Greg's only; panelists' views are excluded.

## Predictions & Notable Claims

### [energy] By 2030 only nuclear-scale power can train a state-of-the-art model
> "Considering the energy need, particularly concentrated energy need for training, is doubling every two years, the expectation by 2030 the only way to train the state-of-the-art model is with a gigawatt supply. So nuclear will be the only way to do so. Unfortunately, the last nuclear reactor we built in America took about 14 years."
> — [01:39:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wiVrlo7XJA0&t=5974s)

**Context:** Greg framing a question to SoftBank's Javier Villamizar during the Stargate fireside; a recurring quantified forecast of his.

### [energy] AI will consume 30% of US energy by 2030
> "Department of Energy report recently said 12%, and DOE is known to take a very conservative approach and they always miss their estimation. So when DOE says 12% of US energy will be consumed on AI by 2028, I think in reality it'll be 30% by 2030."
> — [01:51:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wiVrlo7XJA0&t=6673s)

**Context:** Rapid-fire round of the fireside; Javier had just bet 20-30% of total US energy for AI by 2030 and Greg agreed, going further than the official forecast.

### [energy] Decentralization will unlock small, untapped energy sources
> "Capturing energy is one thing, but transmitting the energy is very hard... a lot of times you may see a source but it may not be big enough to power a data center... but trust me, right now with decentralization of energy and compute along with it, a lot of these untapped sources like geothermal or any of the sources that maybe have small production capability will be used a lot more, and that's the vision that we're going after when it comes to decentralized compute and energy."
