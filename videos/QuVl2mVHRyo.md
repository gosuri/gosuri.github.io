## Summary

A wide-ranging 2026 interview with the DePIN Connection channel. Greg opens with the state of San Francisco — a builder boom he compares to 2012 "at ten times the intensity," oversubscribed hackathons, and teenage builders who all code with AI. He then gives one of his most detailed descriptions yet of his personal "Star Cluster" vision: his own wired smart home running local models on solar power that listen, transcribe, and optimize his life with zero internet dependency, feeding low-latency intelligence to battery-limited home robots (he ordered a 1X robot). He argues privacy makes local AI inevitable, citing the Ring Super Bowl ad and an FBI Nest-camera incident, and says he'd never give OpenAI/Anthropic that level of access to his life.

On energy: training power demand doubles roughly every two years, implying gigawatt-scale training clusters by 2030; the US grid is old and nearly impossible to expand, China adds a nuclear reactor's worth of solar every 36 hours, and the answer is distributed training that taps residential solar. He predicts distributed clusters/training become "a thing" in 6-12 months, that AI agents (unlocked in 2026) make every physically constrained resource scarcer, and that AI at home becomes common within a few years.

Akash specifics: ~60 providers, Star Cluster (home compute as reliable supply) nearly production-ready with a 10-node insider pilot, mutable VM deployments coming for agents, the BME (burn-mint equilibrium) tokenomics overhaul removing USDC so all compute is paid via AKT burns, and an ongoing chain-migration RFP (Ethereum and Solana leading contenders, ~18 foundations pitched). He closes with market-cycle observations: fundamentals-driven DePIN projects lead recoveries, and meme coins are "done for."
