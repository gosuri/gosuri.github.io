## Summary

Duplicate upload (Pundi AI channel) of the identical deAI Summit 2024 talk also posted as CJ-4uolkTeE (Pundi X Labs); transcripts match word-for-word. Greg traces compute history from mainframes through the PC and internet to cloud recentralization (hyperscalers taking ~70% of IT budgets), argues centralization creates a hidden "cloud tax" (~50% of every service dollar; Asana 63%, Palantir ~40%), fragility (CrowdStrike outage), and GPU access gatekeeping (the Semafor Columbia-student story). He warns of "digital feudalism" — invoking Larry Ellison's $100B-plus-nuclear-reactors AI ambitions — where digital lords ration who gets to do AI, and frames decentralized compute as a return to the internet's founding principles, with compute as a public utility like air and water. Closes with Akash adoption stats (Nvidia Brev integration, Nous Research, Prime Intellect, Venice AI, ~27,000 leases last quarter) and Q&A claims that Akash is ~80% cheaper than Amazon with H100s at $1.49/hour vs ~$5.

## Predictions & Notable Claims

### [cloud-decentralization] The cloud tax: half of every dollar goes to cloud providers
> "Today if you use an online service, 50% of every penny you pay goes to one [of] the cloud providers... All of us, no matter what sector we are in, half of every dollar we spend goes to an invisible layer — or the cloud tax, we call it."
> — [00:06:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZhaDoqDXkIk&t=404s)

**Context:** Citing an a16z study of public-company S-1 filings (Asana 63%, Palantir ~40%).

### [decentralized-ai] Without decentralization, we become peasants under digital lords
> "These are the digital lords we are going to be bound to. If we need compute, we need to ask these people to do AI... we're all peasants, and this is the path we're going towards — a future where only wealthy corporations can control access to who gets to do AI. You will be rationed, you will be provisioned, and you will have [to] answer to new digital lords. That's the future we're go[ing] down if [we] don't decentralize sooner."
> — [00:10:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZhaDoqDXkIk&t=622s)

**Context:** The "digital feudalism" thesis, prompted by Larry Ellison's interview about $100B AI training and private nuclear reactors.

### [cloud-decentralization] Compute should be a public utility like air and water
> "Imagine a world where computation is free and accessible as the air we breathe. We believe compute should be a public utility — like air, like water, like all the resources that we don't need to seek someone's permission to use."
> — [00:11:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZhaDoqDXkIk&t=665s)

**Context:** Decentralized compute framed as a return to the internet's founding principles.

### [gpu-economics] Akash is ~80% cheaper than Amazon; H100s at $1.49 vs $5
> "Apples to apples, Akash costs about 80% cheaper than Amazon... With GPUs, in fact, H100 on Akash cost[s] [$1.49] whereas the same resource would cost I think $5 on Amazon."
> — [00:15:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZhaDoqDXkIk&t=929s)

**Context:** Q&A answer on decentralized compute economics; captions render the price as "$149."
