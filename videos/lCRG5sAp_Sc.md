## Summary

First episode of "Conversations with Jack" (Jack Zampolin, early Akash investor and former team member), recorded weeks before Akash Mainnet 2 (Feb 14, 2021 launch, binary freeze Feb 14 noted as "the 14th of February"). Greg recounts his background (AngelHack, Firebase's launch, Kubernetes contributions), why Akash went sovereign-chain after the CryptoKitties congestion showed Ethereum couldn't scale ("if you can't schedule beyond 40,000 users"), and why Tendermint/Cosmos SDK (Golang, pragmatism, deleting ~70,000 then ~100,000 lines of code). Testnet results: 3,800 deployments in two weeks, ~80 apps in the Awesome Akash repo, ~480 monthly active developers (vs. Polkadot's ~420), and 130 Cosmos nodes run on Akash during testing. He details the Thorchain bounty (~$4,000 in RUNE+AKT for a deployment guide) motivated by Thorchain nodes being concentrated on Amazon, the exclusive Equinix Metal launch partnership (bare metal at 2-3x below market), and the IBC vision: stitching Akash compute together with Filecoin/Arweave/Storj/Sia storage, NuCypher KMS, and Handshake DNS into a unified decentralized cloud that can replace incumbents once token/UX friction is solved. Jack (not Greg) predicts a "flood" of smaller data centers joining after production deployments prove out, and explains the Peggy/Gravity Ethereum bridge that would list Cosmos tokens on Uniswap. Same episode also published on the OmniFlix Network channel (21oNz5O1qzk).

## Predictions & Notable Claims

### [cloud-decentralization] Akash as the first open-source cloud
> "That gave way to really building a tool that can leverage Kubernetes and leverage the enormous amount of computing we have to build a second generation, new generation open source cloud — the first open source cloud — and that's really what Akash is."
> — [00:02:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lCRG5sAp_Sc&t=127s)

**Context:** His founding framing: cloud infrastructure was the one layer open source hadn't yet captured.

### [gpu-economics] Launch pricing 2-3x below market on bare metal
> "You're getting high quality, really high quality bare metal servers for next to nothing. I hope these prices will stay the way they are, but I think right off the bat we're looking for like two to three x lower than the market... this is bare metal, so there's nothing in the middle — you're just getting raw performance that is not possible on the cloud today."
> — [00:17:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lCRG5sAp_Sc&t=1024s)

**Context:** On the exclusive Equinix Metal launch partnership for Mainnet 2; Equinix described as the world's largest data center provider, donating testnet compute.

### [cloud-decentralization] Crypto nodes concentrated on Amazon — Thorchain bounty
> "We got picked up by Thorchain... they were having an effort to decentralize because a lot of their nodes are running on Amazon... I reached out to them: look, we can run Thor nodes pretty well, how about doing a thing — and I gave a bounty... about four thousand dollars worth of RUNE tokens and AKT tokens just for writing a guide."
> — [00:12:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lCRG5sAp_Sc&t=769s)

**Context:** Early instance of his recurring "crypto runs on centralized cloud" argument; he calls it "the first time someone else is paying or incentivizing to put their nodes on Akash" [00:14:12].

### [cloud-decentralization] IBC removes the last obstacle to replacing centralized cloud
> "You have to use different tokens and different signatures and all kinds of things — so imagine trying to automate a solution that spans across these networks, like impossible, right? IBC is going to solve that in an amazing way... the real power is when you're going to solve these friction problems for decentralized cloud adoption. Once you solve that, there's nothing in front of us to actually replace incumbents, like centralized cloud providers, in a unified way."
> — [00:22:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lCRG5sAp_Sc&t=1366s)

**Context:** His "interoperable cloud" vision: Akash compute + Filecoin/Arweave/Sia storage + NuCypher KMS + Handshake DNS composed via IBC into a full cloud stack.

### [other] Testnet traction: more active developers than Polkadot
> "When we launched our latest testnet we had 3,800 or so deployments in two weeks... about 80 applications that people shared... and about 480 or so monthly active developers right now on the testnet, which is phenomenal — for comparison, Polkadot has about 420 monthly active developers."
> — [00:09:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lCRG5sAp_Sc&t=593s)

**Context:** Pre-Mainnet-2 metrics; the earlier 2019 incentivized testnet had ~1,900 deployments, 130 providers, growing ~30% week over week [00:07:46].

### [crypto-depin] Pay for compute with any token — the end state
> "Ideally you want to be able to pay for compute — staking is one thing, but actually paying for compute using Ethereum or an ERC-20 token, that means any stable currency... is the ideal scenario that we are all going to [want]. We're going to do this in multiple stages: first we're going to do a wrapped bridge that will eventually become a lot [more] native."
> — [00:29:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lCRG5sAp_Sc&t=1757s)

**Context:** Closing answer on the Peggy/Ethereum bridge and Uniswap listing plans Jack described; multi-token payment later became a shipped Akash feature.
