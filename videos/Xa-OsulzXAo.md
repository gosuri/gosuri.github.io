## Summary

Akash Weekly Twitter Space hosted by Nadia Bajuelo, with Greg's updates followed by a panel of Akash providers (Shimpa, Dami, Huey, and Overclock's Alani Kuye). Greg opens on why he's been coding again ("I love bear markets... fundamentals really speak volumes"), writing guides for deploying Node.js/React apps to Akash without Dockerfiles. Ecosystem updates: Altered app (privacy-optimized messaging) on Akash — prompting his claim that privacy applications will "take center stage"; a Secret Network AKT wrapper (sAKT); Spark IBC; thirdweb (major Ethereum dapp framework) organically testing Akash with deeper integration interest; Blockless integrating from day one; Praetor's new global provider map showing ~50 providers — more regions than Amazon's ~20, feeding his recurring "thousands of regions" vision at 90% below Amazon's cost ("innovation beyond imagination"). He stresses Akash's organic "Powered by Akash" discovery culture (the chain is private; users must self-identify). Cosmos commentary: Kraken/crypto.com/Huobi listing Cosmos assets, Coinbase working through layoffs, compound rumored to move to Cosmos, "interchain is going to be Cosmos," and — if exchange-integration standards (Rosetta) get solved — Cosmos going "way beyond Ethereum." The provider panel covers onboarding experiences (2-3 hours for a non-technical user via Praetor), a Chia farmer repurposing idle plotting hardware as an Akash provider, and provider earnings dashboards. AMA closes with Greg on why he wants the bear market to last, and a detailed answer on stable settlement: rejecting Helium/UST-style burn-mint in favor of a validator-oracle pegged-rate design from the original whitepaper.
