## Summary

"Ask Akash" community-call answer (November 2022) on long-term strategy, which Greg frames as three multi-year phases. Phase one, foundational security: achieved with Mainnet 1 as the first IBC-enabled chain (Cosmos work since 2018, first listing on Osmosis). Phase two, platform parity: shipping the features that let companies move workloads without compromise — launched ephemeral state management first, then persistent storage, next IP addresses, then GPUs, then access control, observability, automation, and managed backends. He's candid that Akash lacks autoscaling, automation, and event streams "and that's okay" — you can't build a cloud from scratch with everything on day one. Phase three, superiority: at parity he expects "at least a thousand different providers," making Akash the most distributed cloud in the world and enabling things clouds can't do (low latency, cloud gaming, open GPU/IP marketplaces). Market sequencing: Cosmos first (~25% of Cosmos already uses Akash by his estimate), then broader web3 (Ethereum/Solana nodes via IP + storage), then machine learning once GPUs land — a massively underserved market with GPUs at 5x list price where Akash can uniquely tap unused general-purpose GPU capacity. He closes with the supercloud thesis (term from Cornell, in Akash's 2020 white paper): a homogeneous cloud abstraction over heterogeneous resources, with Akash as the first commercial implementation — data centers give sovereignty but not scale; supercloud gives both.

## Predictions & Notable Claims

### [cloud-decentralization] A thousand providers will make Akash the most distributed cloud in the world
> "By the time you're done with parity, you can expect at least a thousand different providers... that means [a] thousand different regions. Akash should be the most distributed cloud in the world, and that's fully open, fully permissionless... that gets into possibilities like innovation beyond our imagination — what can you do with thousands of regions across the world? You can do low latency... you could do cloud gaming."
> — [00:03:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IsO-LKTI4sQ&t=221s)

**Context:** The "superiority" phase of his three-phase strategy (security → parity → superiority), stated November 2022.

### [gpu-economics] GPUs at 5x price; Akash uniquely taps unused general-purpose GPU capacity
> "This [is a] massive underserved market that's struggling. I talked [to] people from big cloud providers to small ones... everybody is struggling to get GPUs. GPUs are still 5x price from their market... and Akash is the only way that can tap into unused GPU capacity... these are general purpose GPUs that allow you to run TensorFlow or machine learning applications — the first time an open platform can do that."
> — [00:05:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IsO-LKTI4sQ&t=349s)

**Context:** Pre-GPU-launch (GPUs were next on the roadmap after IP addresses); machine learning framed as the unlock after web3 node workloads.

### [cloud-decentralization] Akash is the first commercial implementation of the supercloud
> "Data centers are great to get sovereignty but they're not good to scale. That's why Akash [has] the idea of a supercloud... where you can have a heterogeneous resource set and give a homogeneous layer that addresses all the functionality of the cloud, from deployment, scheduling, fault tolerance, observability to automation... Akash is the first commercial implementation of a supercloud."
> — [00:07:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IsO-LKTI4sQ&t=479s)

**Context:** Cites the Cornell-coined "supercloud" term and Akash's 2020 white paper; also claims ~25% of the Cosmos ecosystem used Akash in some form at the time.
