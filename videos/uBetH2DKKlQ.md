## Summary

An "Ask Akash" community Q&A clip answering when AKT will list on more centralized exchanges. The speaker is an Akash/Overclock team member — not Greg, whom he references in the third person ("everything that Prashant and Greg covered," "the Gregs and Prashants of the world"). He explains AKT is already on Kraken and KuCoin with the deepest CEX order book, that listing processes are arduous, and that listings alone don't drive volume — product adoption does. He then previews messaging around the "internet tax": citing an a16z analysis that ~50% of cloud-using companies' spend/margins goes to centralized cloud providers (chiefly AWS), so roughly 50 cents of every internet dollar flows to hyperscalers; and frames Hetzner's blanket ban of crypto workloads as evidence that concentrated power is bad for an internet founded on peer-to-peer sovereignty.
