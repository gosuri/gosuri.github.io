## Summary

An informal Akash Twitter Spaces recorded live from Cosmoverse 2022 in Medellin, hosted by Akash community lead Adam Wozny. Guests are Dan (Cosmology, creator of create-cosmos-app), Max (Cloudmos, the Akash deployment/analytics tooling project), Mike (Archway/Phi Labs), and Cosmos OG Ethan Frey (CosmWasm, Wynd DAO). Greg Osuri does not participate. Discussion covers the growth of the Cosmos ecosystem between Cosmoverse editions, the event's collaborative "no-shill" culture, dominant conference themes (liquid staking derivatives, interchain composability, and Sunny Aggarwal's mesh security talk), side events (Osmosis and Archway parties), floated Akash-Juno hackathon plans, and wishes for the next Cosmoverse (front-end talks, hackathons, education tracks, early-project networking).
