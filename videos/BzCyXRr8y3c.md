## Summary

An official Akash Network community livestream hosted by Michael Gushansky (global growth marketing/community) and Maly Ly (Chief Growth Officer), with new head of communications Kelsey Ruiz joining mid-stream. Greg Osuri does not speak; he is mentioned in passing ("Greg loves podcasts," Kelsey's plan to "elevate Greg" through thought leadership, and a note that AngelHack "was one of Greg's babies"). The team recaps bootstrapped growth after Mainnet 2's launch: 111 blogs in 52 weeks, Twitter growth from 800 to 28,000 followers, Telegram from 1,000 to 14,500, three Chinese community summits, 70+ AMAs/interviews, and partnerships with Solana, Kava, Chainlink, Interchain Foundation, and Equinix Metal. Q2 2021 goals: developer/product adoption, expanding AKT liquidity (Gravity DEX testnet promotion, exchange listings), and global awareness via the "four C's" (content, community, communications, campaigns). Teasers include a metaverse gaming promotion, an insiders club, hackathons, a developer portal, a website redesign with merch shop, and a go-to-market sequencing of decentralized-ecosystem projects first, then machine learning/AI workloads. Useful only as historical context for Akash's early 2021 positioning ("world's first open source cloud," "unstoppable cloud"); no statements from Greg himself.
