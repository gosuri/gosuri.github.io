## Summary

Tenth consecutive Akash Weekly Twitter Spaces, hosted by Nadia Bajuelo with guests Abhishek Singh and Siddharth Patil (Comdex) and Maxime Beauchamp (Cloudmos, freshly rebranded from Akashlytics). Greg's update segment highlights Coinbase's web3 developer stack report listing Akash top for decentralized cloud, Hetzner's crypto ban reinforcing Akash's positioning, Spheron's official integration, Cloudmos's new SDL Builder, and Kava's Prop 97 incentivizing builders who deploy on Akash.

The most substantive Greg material is his sketch of fully on-chain public deployments: today's manifest upload is off-chain (private, TCP), but with on-chain manifests a smart contract or DAO (e.g., on Juno) could own and complete Akash deployments — enabling immutable, verifiable, "unstoppable services" and DAO-governed DeFi frontends. He extends this into a thesis that DeFi's future is "full supply chain decentralization" — from decentralized git (Radicle) and contributor identity through deployment, all on-chain — and that a fully decentralized stack is the only escape from a coming censorship regime.

In the AMA he explains proxy servers, the SDL Builder, and how fiat payments (Vexello-style net-30 invoicing) onboard non-crypto businesses without bypassing AKT (fiat buyers still trigger AKT purchases). He claims Akash is ~95% cheaper than traditional cloud with ~50 providers, and expects 1,000-1,500 providers "very soon" once provider incentives ship. He closes with his incentives philosophy: unlike Filecoin's pre-PMF incentives (which he says led providers to pay people to store data), Akash reserves supply-side incentives for post-product-market fit, incentivizing only builders for now.
