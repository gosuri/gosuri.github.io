## Summary

A third-party channel's narrated reading of Greg's opinion piece "Decentralized Infrastructure Allows America to Compete on AI." Greg does not appear on camera or audio, but the entire text is his authored op-ed, read verbatim. The argument: AI is becoming "the bedrock of modern civilization" like electricity and the internet before it, but US infrastructure isn't keeping pace — 2024 US data centers used 200 TWh of electricity, and AI power usage is predicted to reach 165–326 TWh annually by 2028. China's DeepSeek shows the US could "lose the platform war" if it treats infrastructure as an afterthought. His prescription: massive centralized data centers are obsolete single points of failure; instead incentivize distributed infrastructure — smaller compute clusters near localized renewables (solar, wind, geothermal) and idle compute in homes, campuses, and communities — plus federal R&D for distributed computing, opening federal land, and streamlining advanced nuclear. This decentralizes opportunity itself, letting developers "build independently of big tech without begging for compute." Closes with his bio (Akash founder, Overclock Labs CEO, AngelHack founder, IBM, Kaiser Permanente's first cloud architecture in 2008).
