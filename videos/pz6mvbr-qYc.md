## Summary

Identical footage to 0WXiGaybt0M (posted by sister channel Pundi X Labs). At the deAI Summit during TOKEN2049 Singapore, Greg introduces Akash as "the world's first decentralized supercloud" — 50–80% cheaper than Amazon with access to scarce GPUs like H100s — and lays out his three-part framework for blockchain-AI integration: compute coordination (underutilized data centers plus compute in homes, game stations, and home appliances), data coordination (crypto enabling sovereignty and safe sharing of personal data AI can't currently reach), and model coordination (models are only hundreds of lines of code; 40 million developers could write them, not just OpenAI and Anthropic). He compliments Pundi X's data-classification work, then gives his long-term Bitcoin thesis: government overspending and dollar devaluation make scarce assets central, institutions are accumulating, and "Bitcoin is the sea and we're all the boats."

## Topics

decentralized ai, supercloud, gpu marketplace, compute coordination, data sovereignty, open source models, bitcoin, inflation, token2049

## Predictions & Notable Claims

### [local-compute] Untapped compute in homes, game stations, home appliances
> "There's a lot of underutilized compute out there that's just not coordinated. We have underutilized compute data centers, we have compute in homes, game stations, your home appliances and whatnot... and decentralized systems like a blockchain can be used — and is being used right now, Akash is a primary example — to bring that to people."
> — [00:00:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pz6mvbr-qYc&t=46s)

**Context:** First pillar of his blockchain-AI coordination framework; recurring home-compute supply thesis.

### [decentralized-ai] Crypto enables data sovereignty for AI
> "We have a lot of data — you and I, we own our own data — and the AI systems don't have access to all the data that's sitting in our own devices. Is there a good and easy and safe way to share this data? That's where crypto does an incredible job in allowing for sovereignty at the same time access to your data."
> — [00:01:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pz6mvbr-qYc&t=89s)

**Context:** Second pillar: data coordination for AI.

### [open-source-ai] 40 million developers could write AI models
> "A few companies write models, like OpenAIs of the world, Anthropics of the world, but really there are 40 million developers that could essentially write models. Models are not a lot of code — 600 lines or a thousand lines... so decentralizing that intelligence to the people using blockchain is a phenomenal way."
> — [00:02:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pz6mvbr-qYc&t=134s)

**Context:** Third pillar: model coordination — democratizing model authorship beyond a few labs.

### [crypto-depin] Long-term Bitcoin thesis: hedge against runaway inflation
> "I'm terrible at short-term predictions and usually good at long-term predictions... if the government continues to spend like a drunken sailor, I think scarce resources will definitely take like the center stage. Bitcoin is definitely turning out to be the hedge against this runaway inflation — considering that 30% of all the dollars in creation were done in the last three years."
> — [00:03:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pz6mvbr-qYc&t=221s)

**Context:** Asked where Bitcoin goes next year; long-run macro answer, capped by "Bitcoin is the sea and we're all the boats on [it]" at 00:05:09.
