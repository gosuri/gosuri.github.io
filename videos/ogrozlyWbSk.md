## Summary

Panel at Akash Accelerate 2025 moderated by Cheng Wang (COO/CFO of Overclock Labs), with investor Maja Vujinovic (OGroup, ex-GE), Connor Moore (Perian Labs, GPU-backed synthetic dollar), and Mark Palmer (equity analyst, The Benchmark Company). Greg Osuri does not appear on the panel — he gave the event keynote earlier, and the panelists repeatedly reference it (Cheng opens by insisting Akash "never pivoted" to GPUs/AI because it was in the 2018 white paper). Discussion covers what changed in the last 12-18 months: Bitcoin miners pivoting to AI data centers and energy (Hut 8 / American Bitcoin spin-off), cost of capital being far cheaper for AI than Bitcoin, DeepSeek showing training can be ~10x cheaper, and the grid/nuclear constraint creating a huge incentive to solve decentralized training. Panelists debate where value accrues in the AI stack (infrastructure and customer-owning apps as a "barbell," with open-sourced model builders squeezed in the middle), the danger of chat apps like ChatGPT becoming hyper-centralized aggregators exporting culture ("their own local LLMs" as the counter), overrated founder signals, sovereign AI demand in Europe, and how AI/crypto founders should pitch institutional capital. Notable panelist predictions (not Greg's): Maja expects AI to "take over our lives completely" within ~5 years and AI cost of capital to become extremely expensive in five years; Connor expects heavy money thrown at decentralized training and fears OpenAI-level customer centralization; Mark expects more Bitcoin-miner spin-offs and 12-15 year hyperscaler contracts to stabilize the sector. Audience Q&A touches RISC-V and neuromorphic chips.
