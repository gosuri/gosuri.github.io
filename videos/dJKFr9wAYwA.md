## Summary

Akash Weekly Twitter Spaces hosted by Nadia Bajuelo with guests Anthony Rosa (Moultrie Audits, ex-USMC) and Tor (Secret Foundation founder). Greg opens with updates: Mainnet 4 due in about three weeks, which he calls the first-ever open market for IP addresses, with GPUs named as the next mainnet milestone; ecosystem items include Hypersign, terraform support, and Praetor's education program.

The guest segment covers the Department of Defense "Bravo" hackathon, where an app combining Secret Network (private cryptographic key distribution/destruction for military systems) and Akash (proxy access via wallet addresses) won first place for most tactically relevant maintenance-data solution and second for cyber operations. Rosa reveals the first DoD website running on Akash (client-side encrypted, for Second Marine Air Wing), intent to move more sites over, plans to operationalize the app with the NSA, and expectations the DoD would run its own Akash providers.

In the panel on institutional partnerships, Greg contrasts Treasury banning a smart contract (Tornado Cash) with another arm of government embracing decentralization, argues decentralization is a means whose applications matter, and says the DoD spending billions on Amazon for a sovereign deployment platform can be replaced by open source — "open source is clearly winning." He shares that Fortune 50 companies he talks to all have or plan dedicated web3 teams, predicts the bear market clears out "tourists" so the next bull run will be mainstream/enterprise adoption, and looks ahead to interchain accounts enabling private Secret-based deployments on Akash, secure enclaves, and GPU-powered machine learning under privacy. AMA closes with a lukewarm take on Aptos ("faster better Solana... a lot of hype," $54B launch valuation).
