## Summary

Greg's keynote at Blockworks' Crypto x AI summit (June 2025), an earlier delivery of the Star Cluster deck he gave days later at Akash Accelerate 2025. He frames AI as civilization's new fire, targets Kardashev Type One (10 quadrillion watts, ~500x current global energy use), and lays out the energy math: US data centers at 176 TWh in 2023 (4.4% of national electricity, up from ~2% five years earlier), DOE projecting up to 12% by 2028, data centers as 44% of all projected new grid load, and Gartner's warning that 40% of new AI data centers may be unable to secure power by 2026 — with utilities in Texas and Virginia already pausing or denying projects: "the chips are ready, the circuits aren't." His three levers: radical efficiency, massive clean supply (solar, wind, geothermal, micro-nuclear), and decentralized compute — "the lever no one saw coming" — tapping underused servers, edge locations, telco closets, and home rigs. He cites Pluralis, Gensyn (72B on-chain), Bagel (ZK-LoRA), and Nous Research (405B "trained in the wild") as proof decentralized training became "a proven path" in six months, and analogizes: what Bitcoin proved for money, this proves for intelligence. Star Cluster phases: data-center nodes (2025), telco edge, then homes/schools/communities; ~8,300 GB200 NVL72 racks equal a distributed gigawatt. Starbonds offer fractional on-chain ownership of compute ("capital democratization"), workloads migrate to the cheapest/cleanest power, and he repeats his five policy asks. In Q&A he contrasts Akash (permissionless, fully on-chain, open source, self-custody) with Render (closed source, off-chain except payments).

## Predictions & Notable Claims

### [energy] Kardashev Type One needs 500x today's energy; AI is the new fire
> "Every great leap is lit by a new kind of fire. Steam ushered in the industrial age. Electricity powered cities. The internet connected the minds... Now AI is lighting a new kind of fire. To reach [Kardashev] level one, we need 10 quadrillion watts. That's about 500 times our global current energy use."
> — [00:00:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JgD4BzHEcu4&t=4s)

**Context:** Opening framing, identical to his Akash Accelerate 2025 keynote days later.

### [cloud-decentralization] Tomorrow Akash trains and hosts the defining models; the cloud should be owned by all of us
> "Today, Akash runs everything from websites to AI inference, but tomorrow it'll help train and host the models that define the new era. Because the cloud of the future shouldn't be owned by one company — it should be owned by all of us."
> — [00:02:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JgD4BzHEcu4&t=136s)

**Context:** Forward-looking positioning of Akash from inference to training and hosting frontier models.

### [energy] Data centers to 12% of US electricity by 2028; 44% of new grid load
> "According to Department of Energy, by 2028, data centers could use as much as 12% of US electricity. That's more than every home in California combined. We're not just seeing growth, we're seeing a surge. Data centers now represent 44% of all projected new load on the grid."
> — [00:03:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JgD4BzHEcu4&t=181s)

**Context:** Quantified forecasts following the 2023 baseline of 176 TWh (4.4% of national electricity).

### [energy] The chips are ready, the circuits aren't — watts are the new silicon
> "By 2026, Gartner warns that 40% of all new AI data centers may be unable to secure sufficient power to operate. Some utilities are already pushing back — in parts of Texas and Virginia, projects are being paused or denied outright. This isn't because the chips aren't ready. It's because the circuits aren't. We're facing a hard truth: in the age of AI, watts are the new silicon."
> — [00:04:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JgD4BzHEcu4&t=274s)

**Context:** The power-is-the-bottleneck thesis with the Gartner 2026 forecast and current utility pushback as evidence.

### [local-compute] Tap underused servers, telco closets, and home rigs instead of $500M campuses
> "Three, decentralized compute. This is the lever no one saw coming. What if we didn't have to wait for a $500 million hyperscale campus to come online? What if we could tap into underused servers, edge locations, telco closets, and even home rigs? With decentralized compute, we don't need to centralize power. We distribute it."
> — [00:06:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JgD4BzHEcu4&t=363s)

**Context:** Third of three levers to scale AI; home rigs explicitly included in the compute supply.

### [decentralized-ai] In six months decentralized training went from vision to proven path
> "Six months ago, decentralized AI training was a vision. Today, it's a proven path... 405 billion parameters trained in the wild. This isn't a science project. It is a movement... And just like Bitcoin proved for money, this proves for intelligence: we can build better systems by distributing them. Decentralization isn't just viable, it's inevitable."
> — [00:06:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JgD4BzHEcu4&t=406s)

**Context:** Citing Pluralis swarm training, Gensyn's 72B on-chain model, Bagel's ZK-LoRA verification, and Nous Research's 405B model.

### [local-compute] Star Cluster phase three: homes, schools, and communities
> "Phase one is data center grade nodes coming online in 2025... Phase two is telco edge... closer to where data is generated. Phase three is homes, schools, and communities — the long tail, the untapped potential. Each phase expands scale, reach, and ownership... Akash isn't [just] about decentralizing compute. It's about decentralizing who gets to participate in AI's upside."
> — [00:08:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JgD4BzHEcu4&t=536s)

**Context:** The Star Cluster roadmap; ~8,300 distributed GB200 NVL72 racks (~120 kW each) equal one gigawatt of compute without a single building.

### [energy] Compute as a grid-balancing asset following clean power
> "Workloads can migrate in real time to where the power is the cheapest, cleanest, or most available. When it's sunny in Arizona, train there. When the wind is up in Kansas, infer there. This turns compute from a burden into a flexible grid-balancing asset. It's [the] beginning of a new symbiosis between AI and energy."
> — [00:11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JgD4BzHEcu4&t=676s)

**Context:** The energy-aware scheduling thesis, contrasted with static hardwired data centers.

### [crypto-depin] Starbonds are capital democratization for compute
> "We introduce the Starbonds, a digital asset that represents fractional ownership of compute... You reserve a Starbond. A node keeper provisions and operates the hardware. You receive a share of the revenue generated by the node. Transparent, on-chain, and aligned from day one. This isn't just capital formation. It's capital democratization."
> — [00:10:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JgD4BzHEcu4&t=634s)

**Context:** Funding model for Star Cluster, pitched to investors with a QR-code whitelist at the end of the talk.

### [crypto-depin] Akash vs Render: permissionless and on-chain vs closed and off-chain
> "Akash is permissionless, decentralized. It runs fully on-chain — every transaction, every lease happens on-chain — whereas Render is closed source and off-chain. > — [00:14:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JgD4BzHEcu4&t=894s)

**Context:** Q&A answer to "what's the difference between Akash and Render."
