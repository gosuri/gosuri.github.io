## Summary

A #shorts clip from the Web3 Working Group podcast where Greg tells host Amy why he founded AngelHack in 2011: "to take hackathons to the masses." Growing up, his knowledge came from books and from hanging out with older kids who shared his interests — a collaborative culture he found "extraordinary" in India. Moving to the US, he noticed a more introverted culture and wanted to import that collaborative spirit, using hackathons to bring people together ("free pizza, free food... hack for 24 hours"). Purely biographical; no predictions.
