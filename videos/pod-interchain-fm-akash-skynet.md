## Summary

A nearly two-hour conversation with Interchain.FM (host Chjango Unchained) recorded just before Akash Mainnet 6 (GPUs + USDC stable payments). Greg recaps the supercloud lineage — elevating orchestration/fault-tolerance/access control above cloud providers, Akash's 2018 white paper adding the marketplace — and the shift to community-first development (~280 contributors, ~18 Overclock employees, seven independent clients). The GPU thesis: H100s are unobtainable from hyperscalers without special deals (two-year waits from NVIDIA), so Akash becomes the secondary market; he claims access to ~a million GPUs via former ETH-mining fleets, discusses AMD viability (George Hotz/tinygrad rewriting drivers), and previews GPU liquidity-mining ("AKT 2.0") plus a then-unannounced partner to "bring the GPU to the data" — the precursor to devices like Teslas and PlayStations joining the network. The middle hour is speculative: Akash-as-Skynet (the name literally shortens to it), self-replicating AI as the network's biggest future user, AutoGPT already demoed on testnet, proof-of-humanity in consensus, AI-run companies and DAOs coordinating human gig work, healthcare as AI's first dominant application ("a GPT for each person"), open-source models being optimized for cheaper GPUs, and AI's left-leaning RLHF bias. The final third covers SEC overreach (he calls it temporary), FTX/Madoff regulatory capture, NFT lease futures on Akash, and off-record-ish Mainnet 6 timing plus a teased Cosmos-focused LLM trained and launched on Akash. Undiarized two-person transcript; host segments (paperclip maximizer, Mars-governance riff) are excluded from extraction.
