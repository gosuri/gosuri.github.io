## Summary

An "Akash Analysis" clip (speaker uncredited; consistent with Greg — cites "our white paper" and Akash business strategy in first person) on cloud repatriation. The ~$500B cloud industry is "starting to see cracks": Walmart is leaving the cloud and building its own data centers because cloud is too expensive long-term — exactly what the 2018 Akash white paper predicted ("cloud only looks great on the short term but on the long term it's actually very expensive"). The kicker: repatriated data centers built for peak (Black Friday, Christmas) will sit at under 5-10% utilization most of the time, making Akash "their only avenue to offload their capacity." He says Akash will aggressively pursue such companies, is becoming enterprise-ready (private networking, private containers), and predicts a convergence within a couple of years where Walmart-types join Akash both as providers and users.
