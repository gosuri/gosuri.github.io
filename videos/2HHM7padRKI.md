## Summary

Akash Network livestream hosted by community lead Adam Wozney with Zach Cohen (Filebase), Eric Zietlow (Akash devrel), and Colin Lowenberg (Akash partnerships), showcasing winners of the Akash + Filebase hackathon. Greg Osuri does not appear. Winner Rodrigo demos a public library built on Nextcloud deployed to Akash with Filebase (S3-compatible decentralized storage) as the backing store; winner "Space Potato" demos dcloudML, a machine-learning platform that deploys Jupyter/TensorFlow notebooks to Akash, trains an MNIST classifier from Filebase-hosted data, persists models to Filebase, and serves them via TensorFlow Serving on Akash — anticipating future Akash GPU support for deep learning. A third winner (QRShare.io) is mentioned in absentia. Panelists pitch decentralized compute + storage as a business-ready alternative to AWS/GCP/Azure. Notable as an early (pre-GPU, 2021) example of ML workloads on Akash.
