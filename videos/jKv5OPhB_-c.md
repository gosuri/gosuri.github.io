## Summary

Community livestream hosted by Michael (Akash growth/marketing) and community member "Kryptomoth" launching the Akashcraft event — a Minecraft scavenger hunt running on a server deployed on Akash's decentralized cloud, billed as the first Minecraft (and first gaming) server on a decentralized cloud. They walk through the Midgard world map, explain the mechanics (chests hide books containing wallet private keys importable into Keplr/Cosmostation; 1,000 AKT total, average ~7 AKT per chest, range 3–20), PvP rules, ender chests for securing loot, and three starting strategies. Kryptomoth explains his Moth Ruby community for bringing more games (Starbound, Terraria, Steam games) to Akash, and notes his test server was roughly three times cheaper than comparable commercial Minecraft hosting. A Q&A covers server software (Paper Minecraft on an Akash instance, capped at 150 players), Java edition requirements, and event duration, followed by a merch drawing and coordinate release. Greg Osuri does not appear in this stream; it is entirely the marketing team and a community partner. Notable context: they cite ~126 million active Minecraft players and 2.7 billion gamers worldwide as the opportunity for cloud gaming on Akash.
