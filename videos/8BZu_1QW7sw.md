## Summary

Akash's weekly Twitter Spaces hosted by Nadia Bajuelo, with Greg Osuri delivering ecosystem updates alongside Overclock Labs TPM Alani Kuye (Akash accelerator) and community guest Dylan of Evergreen (Chia plot sales built on Akash). Greg covers the upcoming IP address marketplace (validators want it for DMZ-style access control), the Chia plot summer sale — plotting at ~10 cents on Akash versus ~40 cents on traditional cloud and $1-3 per plot at Chia's peak — noting AKT real spend doubled in seven days, and the planned inflation reduction to a 15% max (proposal 21) plus handing the Community Awards Board multisig to community members. Ecosystem wins: WhisperNode moving validators to Akash, Spheron's PaaS beta (deploy from GitHub without touching containers), Cros Nest's live 30-minute node deployment, EVMOS node guides from Chandra Station, and Presearch (150M monthly searches) migrating from Flux to Akash after dropped connections — which segues into Greg's public feud with Flux: he claims he found "massive security holes," notably Flux exposing environment variables publicly (Akash keeps them private between tenant and provider), and that Flux's founder backed out of a proposed live debate. Recurring themes: in a bear market cost becomes a hook and "the signal gets amplified" — he declares Akash "the future of web infrastructure"; web3's AWS dependence ("AWS can cancel web3 with a stroke of a pen"), with ~52% of Osmosis nodes on traditional cloud and Akash now requiring its own validators to run on Akash; and a mention that GPUs/machine learning support is coming. He also reveals he was likely the first LP on Osmosis and has never sold an OSMO token.
