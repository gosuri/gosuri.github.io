## Summary

Official Akash Network walkthrough (presenter: "Caleum," not Greg Osuri) showing how to deploy on Akash using Console Air (air.akash.network) — the non-custodial alternative to the standard email/credit-card Console. Covers connecting a Keplr wallet, minting the $1-pegged ACT stable payment token from AKT, deploying a Tetris template, choosing provider bids by price/location/uptime, managing escrow funding, and closing a deployment — "without an email, without a credit card, and without handing identifying data to anybody."
