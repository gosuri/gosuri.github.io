## Summary

An "Akash Analysis" clip (speaker uncredited; consistent with Greg's voice and framing) reacting to Hetzner's announced ban on all crypto workloads. Hetzner, one of Europe's most cost-efficient clouds, hosted much of the Cosmos ecosystem's node infrastructure and "17 percent of all the cloud nodes for Ethereum." He predicts the ban "will set forth a series of bans by centralized cloud providers" — next "obviously is going to be the Amazons and Googles of the world" — and argues this is exactly why Akash exists: a non-censorable, sovereign, always-on cloud as cheap as Hetzner. He celebrates that Akash trended on Twitter (~7,000 tweets/hour) when the ban hit, calling it a community milestone that Akash was "the first thing on people's mind."
