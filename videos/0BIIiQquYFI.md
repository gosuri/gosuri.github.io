## Summary

Greg's flagship Akash Accelerate 2025 keynote, framing AI as civilization's "new fire" and laying out the most detailed public roadmap yet for decentralized AI infrastructure. He opens with a Kardashev-scale framing (Type One civilization needs ~10 quadrillion watts, ~500x current global energy use), recaps his credentials (Kubernetes/Terraform-era tooling, testifying before Congress on open AI infrastructure), and argues the AI bottleneck is no longer silicon but power: US data centers used 176 TWh in 2023 (4.4% of the grid), possibly 12% by 2028, with Gartner predicting 40% of new AI data centers won't get enough power by 2026. His three levers: radical software efficiency, massive clean supply (solar, wind, geothermal, SMRs), and decentralized compute — the fastest lever, tapping idle capacity in homes, universities, telco closets, and regional colos. He cites Pluralis (asynchronous swarm training), Gensyn (72B model on-chain), Bagel (ZK-LoRA verification), and Nous (405B parameter model trained "in the wild") as proof decentralized training works today. He then announces the Akash Star Cluster roadmap: Phase 1 (2025) data-center-grade "star nodes" in colos plus VMs in August and confidential computing in September; Phase 2 (2026) telco edge (fiber huts, cell towers); Phase 3 (2027) homes, schools, and community sites. AkashML is introduced as a distributed AI runtime (inference today, fine-tuning, then training), and Starbonds as fractional on-chain ownership of star nodes (first issuance ~75,000 bonds at $1,000 par, up to 6.4x ROI claims). He closes with five policy asks (fast-track permits, interconnect reform, tax credits, SMR acceleration, DePIN legal clarity) and the rallying cry "let's build a cloud no one can shut down."
