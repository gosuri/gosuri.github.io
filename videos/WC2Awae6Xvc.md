## Summary

Publicly streamed edition of the normally private Akash Insiders office hours, hosted by community lead Adam Wozney. Content is community operations: new Insider introductions, an icebreaker on books, 2024 event plans (ETHDenver, Nvidia GTC, KubeCon Europe, a Consensus AI meetup), governance proposals (events, hackathons with DoraHacks, community support/Vanguards funding), Zealy campaign results, SIG/working-group updates including the community-built open-source website and stats.akash.network, and a SubQuery data-indexer workshop from guest Boris. Greg Osuri does not speak; he is only mentioned in plugs for an upcoming December 20 year-in-review Twitter Space and a December 21 Insiders AMA with him.
