## Summary

A sub-minute Q&A clip answering how Akash makes money. The speaker (uncredited; answers as an Akash/Overclock principal — "Overclock Labs has tokens and all of us have tokens" — consistent with Greg) corrects the premise: "I'm actually generating revenue right now" — tenants pay providers in tokens for compute. On whether revenue flows to Overclock: on a decentralized platform "there's no single party that makes money, everybody makes money" — value accrues to the token that all participants (including Overclock Labs) hold, plus there's a proposal for "take income," a percentage of hosting fees paid to token holders for staking, similar to Ethereum's staking rewards.
