## Summary

A clip from Orchid Labs' podcast where Greg explains who benefits from decentralizing cloud compute. He opens with his origin story: growing up on a farm in India, learning to code at eight from books, and not owning a computer until college — "there are millions of kids without access to that level of computing." With developer needs growing and data processed "doubling every two years," he sees enormous demand and enormous supply for compute, with no reason middlemen should seek rent in between: ~81% of cloud market share is controlled by four companies in a market headed to a trillion dollars by 2025. Since "everything today goes on the cloud — it is a fabric for today's society," everyone unknowingly pays "an Amazon tax, an invisible tax." Connecting supply directly with demand is, to him, "a dumb problem" whose obvious result is cost reduction — "that's just nature of economics."
