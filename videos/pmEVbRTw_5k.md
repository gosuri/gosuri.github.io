## Summary

Greg's first Cosmoverse keynote, deliberately stepping back from Akash specifics to ask why the decentralization movement exists. He opens with the irony that the internet was born in 1969 as a decentralized network designed to survive nuclear attack, yet four companies now control it. He retells the compute-history arc (mainframes → PCs/mobile decentralizing power → the internet → cloud recentralization), noting ~70% of IT spend goes to hyperscalers, drawing a parallel to late-1800s American monopoly concentration preceding the Great Depression. Familiar evidence follows: the Semafor story of a student who couldn't get GPUs on hyperscalers but got them on Akash (and later founded a company acquired by Nvidia); the a16z "cloud tax" study (half of every penny to online services goes to cloud providers, Asana 63%); the CrowdStrike outage as proof of centralized fragility ("somebody pushed bad code" and grounded the nation's airlines); Amazon FTC antitrust investigations. He warns of "digital feudalism," aggravated by AI's power to influence thought. DePIN (Akash, Filecoin, Sentinel) is framed as restoring internet founding principles and sovereignty. Akash claims: up to 85% cheaper than Amazon (GPUs at 1/4 the cost), four years without downtime vs Amazon's ~200 outages/year, 700% year-over-year growth in user fees, Nvidia as biggest user and the only protocol integrated into Nvidia products. Notably, he says decentralized training has been "cracked": Nous Research's first billion-parameter model was trained on Akash and Prime Intellect's first 10B model was in training on Akash, and he predicts ~5% of global GDP spent on AI by 2030.

## Predictions & Notable Claims

### [cloud-decentralization] The internet's decentralized origins ended with four companies in control
> "Picture this: in 1969 the first and most widely adopted decentralized network was created to withstand a nuclear attack, and today we end up with four companies controlling the internet... Have you really forgotten the very principles that gave birth to this digital age?"
> — [00:00:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pmEVbRTw_5k&t=45s)

**Context:** Opening framing; the talk is a back-to-first-principles argument for decentralization rather than an Akash pitch.

### [cloud-decentralization] The invisible cloud tax on every online service
> "Andreessen Horowitz did this study: half of every penny you spend on online services goes to a cloud provider — in some cases like Asana, where over 63% of all the fees you pay to the cloud service goes to a cloud provider. In a way, we're paying this invisible tax, the society, all of us together — we don't even realize we're paying this invisible tax."
> — [00:05:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pmEVbRTw_5k&t=313s)

**Context:** Recurring quantified claim; followed by the CrowdStrike outage as evidence of centralized fragility.

### [decentralized-ai] Concentration of power plus AI leads to digital feudalism
> "Concentration of power leads to stifling of innovation and will lead to something we call digital feudalism. Feudalistic societies were where few had the power on the many... with this current concentration of power we are very well going back to the feudalism, especially considering the power AI has to control your thought, to influence how you think and what you do."
> — [00:07:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pmEVbRTw_5k&t=443s)

**Context:** His recurring digital-feudalism warning, here extended to AI's influence over human thought.

### [cloud-decentralization] Akash: four years with zero downtime vs Amazon's ~200 outages a year
> "Akash has been around for about four years and has not gone down a single time, and you can't say quite the same for networks like Amazon, where Amazon has reportedly about 200 outages on average per year."
> — [00:10:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pmEVbRTw_5k&t=659s)

**Context:** Reliability argument for peer-to-peer architectures with no single point of failure; also claims up to 85% cost savings and GPUs at 1/4 hyperscaler cost.

### [other] By 2030, ~5% of global GDP will be spent on AI
> "The need for computation, especially for AI, is growing at the rate of half an order of magnitude every year. Today it is predicted by 2030 about 5% of global GDP will be spent on AI."
> — [00:13:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pmEVbRTw_5k&t=789s)

**Context:** Quantified forecast used to explain Akash's growth (700% YoY user-fee growth, Nvidia as biggest user).

### [decentralized-ai] Decentralized training has been cracked, and it happened on Akash
> "AI training has been traditionally very centralized, in the sense that you needed collocated clusters locally present to do any meaningful training. But that changed with Akash. Today we see companies like Nous Research, companies like Prime Intellect, that actually cracked decentralized training. The first billion parameter model by Nous Research was trained on Akash; [the] first 10 billion parameter model by Prime Intellect is being currently trained on Akash."
> — [00:13:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pmEVbRTw_5k&t=834s)

**Context:** October 2024 claim that decentralized training moved from theory to practice, with Akash "front and center."
