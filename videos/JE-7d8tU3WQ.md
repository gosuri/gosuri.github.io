## Summary

Coin Republic interview with Greg at Token2049 Singapore. Greg recounts his path: ~25 years programming, cloud-native open source (Kubernetes-era tooling used by AWS, Docker, and the Department of Defense), and founding AngelHack, which he credits with defining the modern hackathon and launching Firebase. Seeing hackathon developers unable to scale deployments, and data centers 85-90% underutilized, he built Akash on the "supercloud" idea from a Cornell paper, adding a marketplace layer — noting the name in Sanskrit means "sky" and translates to "Skynet." He describes GPU scarcity (Musk's "GPUs are harder to get than drugs," Larry Ellison begging Jensen Huang over dinner), idle GPUs at mining and ML companies, and enormous compute distributed in home devices like gaming consoles that needs a federated system to harness. Akash is 80% cheaper than the hyperscalers and permissionless — he argues compute should be "as free as the air we breathe." He covers the CloudMOS and Praetor acquisitions, integrations with Nvidia (the only crypto protocol integrated into Nvidia products), Prime Intellect (decentralized training), and Venice AI — sharing personal stories of using Venice for a private medical diagnosis and newborn questions. On sustainability, he says AI's energy needs can't be met by solar and "we're going to have to go nuclear," with decentralized tech the best bet for efficiency. The 5-year vision: 10x growth, parity with the cloud, transitioning Akash from a resource network to a services network with thousands of regions vs. Amazon's 32.

## Predictions & Notable Claims

### [local-compute] Enormous idle compute sits in our home devices; it needs federation
> "A lot of GPUs — talk about gaming consoles in our homes, right? My gaming console, I use them once a day. There's enormous compute in our devices that is just distributed everywhere. That's why we need a system that can take advantage of this distributed compute and bring them in a federated manner — that's what Akash is."
> — [00:07:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JE-7d8tU3WQ&t=439s)

**Context:** Explaining why idle GPU capacity (miners, ML companies between training runs, consumer devices) demands a federated marketplace.

### [cloud-decentralization] Compute should be as free as air and water
> "In a world where we have heavy censorship, having a layer that is considered to be the public utility — right, compute — should be as free as the air we breathe and water we drink, and that is a foundation that humanity can accelerate on, and that foundation needs to be permissionless."
> — [00:08:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JE-7d8tU3WQ&t=483s)

**Context:** The third and "most important" benefit of Akash after GPU access and 80%-cheaper cost: permissionless compute as public utility.

### [energy] AI will have to go nuclear
> "Especially with AI, the need for electricity and compute — I don't think we'll be able to even fulfill with solar or any of the sustainable methods. I think we're going to have to go nuclear for powering AI."
> — [00:18:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JE-7d8tU3WQ&t=1090s)

**Context:** Sustainability discussion; he adds that decentralized technologies "have the best bet" at making new AI data centers sustainable and energy efficient, and that Akash is sustainable by definition since it reuses existing compute.

### [cloud-decentralization] Five-year vision: cloud parity and a services network with thousands of regions
> "The goal is to get parity with the current cloud... Akash will transition from a resource network to a services network where you have hundreds if not thousands of services all offered in a decentralized manner... The question I'll leave you folks with is: what happens when you have thousands of regions that are spread across the world with thousands of services? Amazon has 32 regions right now — imagine that as a thousand regions."
> — [00:19:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JE-7d8tU3WQ&t=1179s)

**Context:** Answer to "how do the next 5 years look?"; he also cites 50% quarter-over-quarter growth in on-chain leases and a goal to 10x growth.

### [gpu-economics] Data centers are 85-90% underutilized
> "As we were deploying a lot of these solutions to different data centers across the world, we realized most compute is underutilized — somewhere north of 85% to 90% of the time, data centers have underutilized compute, unused compute, because you need peak capacity to add peak workload."
> — [00:03:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JE-7d8tU3WQ&t=182s)

**Context:** Origin story of Akash; this underutilization stat is one of his most repeated founding claims.

### [decentralized-ai] GPU scarcity even for the richest; idle GPUs are the answer
> "Elon Musk is famously known for saying how hard it is to get GPUs — him being the richest man of the world — he said something in the lines of GPUs are harder to get than drugs... Larry Ellison was last week talking about how he had dinner with Jensen Huang, the CEO of Nvidia, and literally begging him... to give GPUs, and Jensen wouldn't give because he just don't have them."
> — [00:06:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JE-7d8tU3WQ&t=397s)

**Context:** Illustrating that GPU demand outstrips supply so badly that even the wealthiest can't buy them, motivating Akash's marketplace for idle GPUs.
