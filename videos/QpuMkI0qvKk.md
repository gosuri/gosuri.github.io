## Summary

Akash Accelerate 2025 talk by Manouk Termaaten, founder/CEO of Vertical AI, a no-code AI model customization platform running on decentralized compute. Traction since November 2024: 25,000+ users, 10,000 MAU, 100K prompts in 30 days, 40+ models. He shares two AWS benchmarks: hosting DeepSeek R1 cost $28K/month on AWS vs $16.5K on Akash (41% cheaper), and a later 14-day, seven-machine test cost $7.1K on AWS vs $1.7K using Akash plus Vertical's own orchestration layer (75% cheaper), which adds autoscaling, load balancing, on-demand job scheduling, and LoRA-adapter multiplexing to serve many customers per machine. Greg does not appear in this video.
