## Summary

A technical tutorial by Logan Cerkovnik of Thumper AI (an Akash partner), not Greg Osuri. He walks through deploying vLLM (a high-throughput, OpenAI-API-compatible LLM server) on Akash via the console: choosing quantized models (AWQ WizardLM-2 7B), accepting 4090/A100/H100 bids (citing ~$1.40/hr A100s and ~$2/hr H100s versus ~$20/hr on AWS), running Open WebUI as a private multi-user ChatGPT replacement "for less than $500 a month," and wiring LangChain/CrewAI agents to the endpoint via Jupyter. Useful as a snapshot of Akash's 2024 AI-inference developer experience, but contains no statements from Greg.
