## Summary

An Akash Twitter Spaces recorded live from Devcon 2022 in Bogota, Colombia, hosted by Adam Wozney (community lead at Overclock Labs) — Greg Osuri does not participate. Guests are Conor O'Day (Gitcoin, on quadratic funding and public goods), Edo (Vega Protocol perception team), and Evin McMullen (CEO of Disco, on decentralized identity). Discussion covers Devcon themes: bear-market conferences being builder-focused rather than speculative, Ethereum staking debates (Lido vs. Rocket Pool), censorship resistance after the Tornado Cash sanctions and OFAC-compliant block building, account abstraction, sybil resistance (Gitcoin Passport), and inclusivity at the event. An audience member asks about the Akash GPU marketplace timeline; Adam redirects him to Greg's weekly Wednesday Twitter Spaces and the public roadmap rather than answering. No statements by Greg appear in the video.
