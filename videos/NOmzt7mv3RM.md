## Summary

Interview recorded at Consensus's 10th anniversary. Greg introduces Akash as "the world's first supercloud" — a cloud that connects any cloud-grade compute, whether in a data center, a home, or a private/public setting, at roughly 1/10th of Amazon's price because supply comes from underutilized resources. He stresses Akash is the only network, decentralized or centralized, offering on-demand H100s at reasonable prices, sourced from ML companies sitting on idle H100/A100 fleets after upgrading. He frames GPU scarcity as fundamentally a coordination problem that blockchains solve well. He recounts the origin story: Overclock Labs began in 2015 to take Kubernetes to market for edge computing, adopted a blockchain for permissionless coordination around 2016, started with proof-of-work, then moved to proof-of-stake on its own Cosmos-lineage layer 1 (the first chain to complete an IBC transaction with Cosmos Hub). A recurring argument: tokens give open source software sustainability — Akash's ~500 contributors and full-time teams are funded directly from the protocol — and tokenized open source will become the third open source business model after Red Hat's support model and the open-core model (Databricks). On security, he describes Akash as "Airbnb for data centers" (~80 providers at the time) with a decentralized network of auditors posting compliance proofs on-chain, arguing this gives more visibility than AWS. Final segment covers blockchain for content provenance against deepfakes (device-level signing, adoption is the hard part). He closes urging people to get past crypto stigma and try Akash for GPUs.

## Predictions & Notable Claims

### [cloud-decentralization] Akash as the world's first supercloud, spanning data centers and homes
> "Akash is the world's first supercloud. What a supercloud is, is essentially a cloud that connects any cloud-grade compute — be it in a data center, be it in a home, or be it in a private setting or a public setting — and brings [it] together in a way that's easily consumable by developers around the world."
> — [00:00:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NOmzt7mv3RM&t=2s)

**Context:** Opening answer to "what are you working on"; the supercloud framing explicitly includes home compute as first-class supply.

### [gpu-economics] GPU scarcity is a coordination problem blockchains solve
> "There's obvious crazy demand for these chips, but there [is] also just supply, and turns out the only problem is really coordination — and blockchains are phenomenal coordination networks. In doing so we created the world's first open source cloud."
> — [00:02:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NOmzt7mv3RM&t=130s)

**Context:** After citing Nvidia demand, Elon Musk's "easier to get drugs than Nvidia chips" line, and hundreds of ML companies sitting on idle H100/A100 fleets.

### [crypto-depin] Tokenized open source will be the third open source business model
> "I think tokenized open [source] software, it's going to be a third leg of open source business models... You have Red Hat which mastered the support model... then you have the open core model, which is Databricks being a popular one... but Akash is pure open source, there's no commercial aspect... and I think the token [in] open source software, it'll be a third leg in open source business models."
> — [00:06:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NOmzt7mv3RM&t=390s)

**Context:** Recurring thesis: tokens' second-order effect is sustainability for open source (Akash cited as category creator for DePIN, with ~500 contributors funded from the protocol).

### [cloud-decentralization] Airbnb for data centers with trust via decentralized auditors
> "The best analogy for Akash is Airbnb for data centers... you have about 80 providers now, all different sizes and shapes... and the way you know someone is telling the truth is through auditing. There is a decentralized network of auditors... [that] verify this compliance offline and post a proof online... Akash gives you more trust than traditional Amazon because Amazon will not show you the certificates."
> — [00:07:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NOmzt7mv3RM&t=475s)

**Context:** Answer to the classic enterprise-security objection to decentralized cloud; claims proof-backed visibility beats hyperscaler opacity.

### [decentralized-ai] AI on blockchain is a reality now
> "I highly recommend folks go to Akash Network, [get] GPUs, and proof is in the pudding. And I think, you know, AI on blockchain is a reality now, and blockchains are definitely saving AI."
> — [00:13:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NOmzt7mv3RM&t=814s)

**Context:** Closing remark; echoes his earlier reference to a Semafor story calling blockchain "AI's biggest savior" for GPU access.
