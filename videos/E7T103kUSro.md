## Summary

A screencast demo (narrator uncredited, not identified as Greg) of the Akash Deploy Tool, a community tool created by "Space Potato." Walks through the deployment flow: installing the Keplr wallet, choosing an SDL ("a recipe the provider will use to create the resources") from a pre-configured gallery, creating an on-chain certificate, posting a deployment request, choosing among provider bids, taking out a lease, sending the manifest, and opening the deployed app's URL (a LunaLite crypto wallet).
