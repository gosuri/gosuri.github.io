## Summary

Data-heavy talk at Akash Accelerate 2025 by Jason Badeaux, co-founder/CEO of Daylight (a crypto-incentivized distributed-energy utility), invited by Greg to speak about energy. Key points: energy use and GDP per capita are inseparable; US electricity demand is booming for the first time in decades (0% to 4.7% annual growth, $21T capex needed by 2050); FERC's peak-demand forecasts keep being revised upward (23 GW → 39 GW → 3x); Vistra (an energy producer) outperformed Nvidia in the 2024 market because "power is the constraint on compute"; the US grid got a D+ (60% of transformers past useful life), interconnection queues stretch 5+ years for power plants and reportedly 10 years for new data centers; transmission build rates are ~10-20x short of DOE targets. His pitch: distributed energy (rooftop solar plus batteries, networked and dispatched as virtual power plants) deploys in weeks instead of years, and crypto incentives can attack the industry's 60% customer-acquisition-cost problem and financing costs. He closes by citing Greg's "Akash at home" post and calling decentralized energy plus decentralized compute "one of the most exciting intersections in the world." Greg does not speak in the video.
