## Summary

Solana Breakpoint 2023 talk by the Render Network Foundation (with a recorded hello from founder Jules Urbach) covering the network's film-industry roots in distributed GPU rendering, its live migration to Solana (new RENDER SPL token, burn-mint model, incentive proposals), repositioning as a broad compute platform with AI compute clients (io.net, Beam Cloud), 2023 metrics (9M+ frames, ~200K jobs), and new high-compute job types — Las Vegas Sphere displays, Apple Vision Pro stereoscopic content (8–50x), and light fields (up to 200x). Relevant to the decentralized-GPU competitive landscape around Akash, but Greg Osuri does not appear and is not quoted.
