## Summary

A short segment from the Akash Accelerate 2025 keynote introducing AkashML, "a distributed AI runtime designed to unlock real performance from globally scattered resources" — GPU-native, container-first, abstracting hardware heterogeneity so developers deploy models across worldwide nodes without per-machine quirks. Roadmap: "inference today, fine-tuning tomorrow, and fully training soon after," with a seamless experience "whether you're running a star node in Austin or a micro grid in Nairobi." Pitch points: usage-based pricing, hardware transparency, plug-and-play open model hubs, and an "open model lifecycle" — unlike platforms that silently remove unprofitable models, AkashML surfaces real-time costs so communities can attract usage or self-host to keep models alive; per-instance rate limiting prevents noisy-neighbor latency. (Keynote segment at Akash's own conference; presenter consistent with Greg's keynote announcing AkashML.)
