## Summary

A step-by-step Akash Console deployment tutorial narrated by "Calum" (not Greg Osuri). It reflects the post-BME (burn-mint equilibrium) era: the intro describes Akash as a marketplace putting "the world's spare computing resources... in the hands of any human or AI agent," fully automated and permissionless, with providers ranging from certified data centers (on-chain reputations, audited attributes, optional SLAs/legal contracts) to home labs. The walkthrough covers both login paths — Cosmos wallet (Keplr) for permissionless AKT deployment, and email/credit-card signup (a $100 free trial, 24-hour deployment limits until a $20 deposit makes credits permanent) — deploying a Tetris/Mario game template through the reverse-auction bid flow, the new ACT deployment credit system (minting ACT from AKT via the burn-mint page; ACT converts back to AKT while card-purchased credits don't), auto-top-up for card accounts, the SDL builder and community template gallery, and management tabs (leases, alerts, logs, shell, events, update). Useful as a snapshot of Akash's 2026 UX and tokenomics rather than for any statements by Greg.
