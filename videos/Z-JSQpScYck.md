## Summary

The earliest video in the archive: a 2015 screencast where Greg demos Overclock's cluster-management features — launching a Rails+Postgres cluster into a chosen data center, namespaces for multi-tenant isolation ("better than conventional dedicated machines because it promotes better sharing of resources... a way more efficient system"), telemetry dashboards, a 5,000-request load test, VPN access to private resources, and tearing the cluster down to show the "disposable nature of the infrastructure." Historically notable: he introduces "Akash" as "an internal tool we created to manage Overclock itself" — the first appearance of the name that later became the network. Pre-blockchain era; no crypto or marketplace concepts appear.
