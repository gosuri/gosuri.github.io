## Summary

Akash Weekly Twitter Spaces hosted by Nadia Bajuelo, with Greg, Spheron co-founder/CEO Prashant Maurya, and Overclock Labs CFO Cheng Wang. Greg opens with updates: Mainnet 4 is weeks away with IP leases as the headline feature (enabling unique IPs/ports for workloads like Solana, Ethereum, and Bitcoin full nodes), it is the last big release before GPU support ("hopefully the end of the year"), and he teases a mystery platform announcement at the Messari Mainnet conference the next day. Prashant describes Spheron's mission (decentralized front-end and, via Akash, full-stack/node deployments), its India-first community growth, and a coming node marketplace. Cheng gives a long explainer on the September AKT token unlock — ~20M tokens, mostly to foundation/ecosystem wallets, ~8M to investors who he argues have no economic incentive to sell — plus bearish macro commentary (expects equities/crypto bottom around Q4 2022–Q1 2023 on disappointing corporate earnings) and cites a16z's analysis that ~50% of internet-company spend/margin goes to centralized cloud providers, an "internet tax" Akash aims to abolish (Cheng, not Greg). Greg closes with portfolio-on-autopilot/DCA remarks (mentions buying bitcoin at $54k and $18k), argues ecosystem partners like Spheron will eventually replace the core team as the primary growth engine, claims ~20% of the Cosmos ecosystem uses Akash with a goal of 80% within 12 months, and says the Ethereum Merge validates Akash's early bets on proof of stake, Cosmos, and Kubernetes. A garbled passage suggests an organic adoption story from the prior week (captions render it "DoD") driven by ecosystem partners rather than the core team.
