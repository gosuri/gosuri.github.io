## Summary

An Akash Weekly Twitter Spaces hosted by Nadia Bajuelo with guest Rebecca Liao, co-founder/CEO of Saga (automated deployment of app-specific "chainlets" via interchain security), plus Greg Osuri's weekly network update and Q&A. Rebecca covers Saga's chain-to-launch-chains model, gaming/entertainment focus, hidden backend token design, and post-Luna fault-isolation arguments for the Cosmos sovereign-chain thesis; she cites Akash as compute for Saga validators and for games like Passage 3D and Strange Clan.

Greg's update reports all-time highs: 1,000 AKT daily spend, 50,000 AKT cumulative spend, over 10 TB of active memory, ~77 TB of active storage, and roughly 450 active providers. He frames marketplace health in terms of liquidity and fill rate, claiming ~30% utilization of memory supply versus Filecoin's ~0.7% storage liquidity, and attributes demand growth to Chia miners plotting for 10 cents vs 40 cents on cloud. He announces inter-chain accounts as code-complete, describing a Juno DAO use case where users deploy to Akash paid in JUNO without realizing Akash is underneath, plus Secret Network "ownerless" anonymous deployments and the upcoming IP leases marketplace. On adoption strategy he lays out the sequencing that would define Akash's next two years: crypto-native first (product-market fit achieved), then broader Web3/Ethereum, then "computation heavy workloads... when we have GPUs — things like machine learning" with instant cost savings, before ever attacking Web2. He sketches a future of 5,000 providers in 5,000 locations enabling globally distributed low-latency high-performance compute "impossible on the cloud," notes staking 400 AKT effectively earns a free server monthly, states Overclock Labs makes no money on Akash, and closes predicting an "inter-chain future" where protocols compose rather than build everything themselves.
