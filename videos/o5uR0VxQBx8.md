## Summary

Twitter Spaces hosted by Adam Wozney (Akash community lead) announcing the Akash-Coldstack partnership, with Eric Zeitlow (Akash developer relations) and Justin Silver (Coldstack communications). Greg Osuri does not speak — he is only mentioned in passing regarding an earlier year-end product-update Spaces. Coldstack is described as a decentralized cloud storage aggregator (S3-compatible portal over Filecoin, Arweave, Sia, Crust, Storj) specializing in "cold data"; Akash provides the compute side ("peanut butter and jelly"): Coldstack deploys file nodes on Akash for retrieval and scaling, while Akash users gain S3-style storage endpoints Akash doesn't natively offer. Eric outlines Akash's 2022 roadmap — an incentivized testnet and, notably, GPU support targeting gaming, AI/ML and mining workloads — plus community programs (Akash Insiders, community awards board). Q&A covers web2-to-web3 adoption strategy (crypto projects first, enterprises later), AKT as a compute-backed utility token usable without credit cards or banks, Coldstack's CLS tokenomics, and closing thoughts on web3 collaboration over competition.
