## Summary

Conference talk at Akash Accelerate 2025 by Dillon Rolnick of Nous Research — Greg Osuri does not appear or speak. Rolnick argues AI is at a fork between closed-lab AGI and open-source models, critiques "how to make a bomb" and "systemic bias" flavors of alignment (citing Anthropic model-snitching anecdotes, Grok's South Africa tangents, Gemini's image fiascos), traces the effective-altruism/OpenAI/Anthropic founding lineage, and pitches Nous's answer: the Psyche network (pre-training a 40B-parameter model on ~500B tokens over a truly global distributed run, enabled by a 4,000x bandwidth reduction, with checkpoints uploaded to HuggingFace) and the Atropos open-source reinforcement-learning framework (runnable on an Akash node). He claims ~8,000 idle H200s sit on decentralized platforms — enough to retrain DeepSeek R1 in 500 hours with half of them — and that crypto provides the "economic substrate to own AI itself" as an alternative to closed-lab UBI visions. Closing line: "Skynet was a closed source company." Relevant to the archive as ecosystem context for Greg's decentralized-training thesis (he cites Nous's work elsewhere), but no Greg statements to extract.
