## Summary

Community livestream hosted by Adam Wozney announcing Akash's support for the Sovryn hackathon (June 4 - July 18, 2021, $100K in AKT), with Greg Osuri and Sovryn's exiledsurfer. Greg opens with metrics: adoption grew 40% week-over-week in active deployments (over 4,000 total applications deployed), and his two daily obsessions are deployments going up and time-to-deploy coming down — targeting a drop from ~90 minutes to two minutes. He teases Bitcoin Miami sponsorship and his "Faces of Crypto" photo-book project, and says Gravity DEX (where ATOM/AKT was the top liquidity pool in a ~25,000-participant testnet) will deploy on Akash mainnet soon. The middle section is a wide-ranging conversation: exiledsurfer recounts Sovryn staying online during the "wrecking ball Wednesday" crash while Coinbase and Binance went down; both discuss decentralization as an architectural pattern rather than dogma, cloud and telecom oligopolies (Greg: four companies control 81% of the cloud), and crypto as "a great equalizer." Greg highlights Althea decentralizing 5G with its core running on Akash, Helium running testnets on Akash, and Skynet/Sia + Akash + Handshake interoperability patterns, predicting Akash powers 5G in three to four years. Hackathon challenges: (1) deploy anything anywhere but AWS ("they're just bad people"), (2) combine Akash compute with decentralized storage (Sia over Filecoin, in his view), (3) build permissionless identity/auth on Akash. Q&A covers Gravity DEX liquidity, a coming no-code deployment future, and "what stops AWS from copying Akash" — Greg's answer: "if AWS does what Akash does, we won," since open systems historically always win. Ends with prize wheel giveaways.
