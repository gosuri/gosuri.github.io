## Summary

A short walk-up interview at Cosmoverse Dubai 2024. Greg defines the Akash "supercloud" as "a cloud of clouds" aggregating idle data centers and cloud-capable computers worldwide, offering compute at one-tenth of Amazon/Google/Microsoft prices — especially scarce AI GPUs like H100s. He claims Akash was first in its category and "created this new category called DePIN," citing ~1700% year-over-year growth in user fees and workloads doubling in a year. Asked about future impact, he delivers one of his signature forecasts: by 2030 about 5% of global GDP will be spent on AI compute, and the question is whether four companies or users control it — "if we don't own the AI, we will be owned by the companies that give us AI." On AI fear, he's optimistic: AI "buys you time" rather than replacing humans — as a CEO he now codes again because AI makes it fast. Closes with "the future of money is decentralized and self-sovereign and permissionless."
