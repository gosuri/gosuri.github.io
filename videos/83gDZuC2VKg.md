## Summary

An Akash community office-hours session (recorded on Discord, hosted by Adam from the community team) featuring Cheng Wang from Akash's finance side — Greg Osuri does not speak. The session walks through the Chandra Station governance proposal to reduce AKT's inflation band: max from 40% to 25%, min from 25% to 15%, versus the then-current ~33.2% APR (Keplr's ~45% figure reflects compounding APY). Cheng explains Cosmos SDK inflation mechanics — the floor/ceiling band, the target bonded ratio (~67%) versus actual (~70%), and how inflation trends toward the floor when bonding exceeds target. He frames the rationale: AKT is at least 10 million tokens oversupplied versus the whitepaper projection, initial high inflation was meant to bootstrap the network in lieu of utility, and inflation should fall as adoption rises. The proposal also preempts the "dynamic inflation decay curve" feature slated for testnet 3 and Mainnet 3 (then estimated one and a half to two months out), which would auto-adjust inflation to the whitepaper curve without further governance proposals. Audience questions cover the discontinued "Akash Supermini" home-compute device (a prototype "mini data center" that would earn AKT by hosting workloads — shelved indefinitely because network demand wasn't at the scale to utilize it), exchange listings (Cosmos SDK tokens require custom integrations unlike ERC-20s), whether reduced issuance cuts sell pressure (likely, though unquantified), and take rate (not yet implemented, to come via future governance). Notable dated tidbits: PKT onboarding drove a demand spike, and a follow-up session on escrow and the inflation curve was teased.
