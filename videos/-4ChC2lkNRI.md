## Summary

A short DePIN panel at a Cosmos event, with Greg acting as moderator alongside Dimitri (Sentinel, decentralized VPN/bandwidth marketplace) and Michael O'Rourke (Pocket Network, decentralized RPC). Greg introduces himself as co-founder of Akash, "the world's first decentralized supercloud" that leverages underutilized compute to offer significantly cheaper compute permissionlessly, then largely steers the discussion: what DePIN means to each panelist, why infrastructure needs decentralization, why they chose Cosmos, and closing alpha. Michael defines DePIN as incentivized physical infrastructure where geography matters, recounts the Tornado Cash RPC-censorship incident as a concrete case for permissionless demand, notes Pocket serves 300–500M RPC requests/day and argues DePIN networks are often not cheaper in pure cost terms — tokens subsidize infrastructure redundancy — but can be cheaper to the end user. He predicts censorship-resistant protocols become increasingly important over the next three to five years (his claim, not Greg's). Dimitri covers Sentinel's dVPN privacy model, open-source verifiability, and upcoming dynamic node pricing and faster blocks. Both panelists explain choosing Cosmos (IBC, CometBFT, small-team blockchain tooling). Greg contributes framing but makes no extended arguments or predictions of his own beyond his Akash intro.
