## Summary

Akash community livestream hosted by Adam Wozney with Colin Lowenberg (Akash dev relations/BD), Derek Anderson (Akash engineer), and Daniel Helm (Skynet Labs developer evangelist) covering the Akash-Skynet partnership. Colin gives an Akash primer (permissionless compute marketplace, ~1/3 the price of AWS, aimed at breaking the Amazon/Google/Microsoft "triple monopoly"); Daniel introduces Skynet as a layer-2 sharing layer over Sia's decentralized storage. They present the "unstoppable web" stack — Handshake for DNS, Skynet front ends, Akash back ends, databases backed up to Skynet — plus demos: git LFS storage on Skynet, a React app deployed to a Skynet portal, and the hackathon leaderboard whose scraper runs on Akash. They announce a $10,000 Akash-Skynet integration challenge and mention OpenRegistry, a decentralized Docker Hub alternative on Akash. Greg Osuri does not appear (he is referenced only as Colin's friend and via a meme image in the demo).
