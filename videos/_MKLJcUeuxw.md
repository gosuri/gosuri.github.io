## Summary

An Akash Network customer case study: community manager Caleum interviews Alon Berg, co-founder of Astria, a platform for fine-tuning and running generative image models for fashion/e-commerce (AI photo shoots, headshots, avatars). Berg recounts Astria's infrastructure journey from Lambda Labs to AWS (too costly, slow provisioning) to RunPod (unreliable, slow networking) before settling on Akash over a year ago for H100/H200 pricing and Kubernetes-based providers with pre-cached docker images. He discusses their pivot after models like Nano Banana reduced fine-tuning demand, their new super-resolution-with-reference model, and how Akash pricing let them retain price-sensitive customers. Greg Osuri does not appear in this video.
