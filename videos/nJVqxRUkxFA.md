## Summary

Weekly Twitter Spaces hosted by Nadia Bajuelo with guest Julian Morales (head of technology at Vexillo, a Kusama/Algorand validator building on Akash). Greg's updates: Ali Merchant (ex-Ignite/Tendermint) joins as head of DevRel; new projects hosting on Akash (Stakewolle validator, money.space — "curve for IBC"); Spheron enabling multi-currency settlement targeting the Polygon/Ethereum ecosystems; Praetor announcing a deployment UI alongside Cloudmos (which won ~$98k in on-chain community funding); dVPN vs. self-hosted VPN tradeoffs; and airdrops for AKT stakers (Defund). His most forward-looking idea: fully on-chain, DAO-owned deployments — smart contracts owning workloads with Handshake-based DNS discovery, enabled by interchain accounts — so deployments become autonomous and ownerless, and "you can't take down a smart contract, that means you can't take down a deployment either." Vexillo's segment covers a fiat on-ramp for SMEs: pay by card/bank via Singapore banks, receive tax-claimable invoices, with "compute credits" provisioning AKT behind the scenes. In the AMA Greg addresses viral FUD claiming Akash providers run on Amazon: the snapshot captured Praetor test providers, and his own analysis (a tool deploying to all ~50 providers every 10 minutes with reverse-IP lookups, promised "in a week or two") showed no Amazon providers — "why would you host on Amazon and provide the same compute for 97 percent cheaper?"

## Predictions & Notable Claims

### [cloud-decentralization] Autonomous, ownerless deployments no one can take down
> "You can imagine a world where a DAO, or a smart contract, actually completely owns the deployment without human intervention — so that removes the risk of the human being vulnerable... now we can have programs owning other programs and hosting those programs... I'm really getting excited about this idea that we can fully have autonomous deployments, ownerless deployments — essentially no single human being owning a deployment, fully running autonomously — and they cannot be taken down, because you can't take down a smart contract, that means you can't take down a deployment either."
> — [00:03:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nJVqxRUkxFA&t=215s)

**Context:** Sparked by conversations with the Juno team about fully on-chain deployments using interchain accounts and Handshake DNS discovery; he calls it "a new era of decentralized cloud that the world has never seen" [00:04:18]. Prefigures his later AI-agent/autonomous-infrastructure themes.

### [crypto-depin] Infrastructure airdrops are coming
> "We saw Defund Finance said they're airdropping to AKT holders [and] stakers... highly suggest you stake your AKT if you have them lying around... I think we're going to see quite a bit of these airdrops for infrastructure projects."
> — [00:05:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nJVqxRUkxFA&t=300s)

**Context:** Ecosystem update; he also mentions Passage 3D/Strange Clan as likely airdrops.

### [cloud-decentralization] No Akash providers on Amazon — it's 97% cheaper here
> "I've looked at preliminary data — I haven't seen anyone running on Amazon at all, because it does not make sense. Like, why would you host on Amazon and provide the same compute for 97 percent cheaper? It makes no sense... the real data indicates there are no Amazon providers."
> — [00:31:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nJVqxRUkxFA&t=1863s)

**Context:** Rebutting a viral graphic claiming Akash providers ran on AWS; he explains the data captured Praetor test providers (Akash lacked a testnet), and describes the verification system he built over a weekend — deploying to all ~50 providers every 10 minutes with reverse-IP lookups — with a full report promised in one to two weeks.
