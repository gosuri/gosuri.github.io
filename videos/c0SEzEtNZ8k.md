## Summary

An Akash Weekly Twitter Spaces hosted by Nadia Bajuelo with guest James Friel (BD Director at Axelar) and an extended Greg Osuri update/AMA. Greg previews Mainnet 4 (two weeks out): the world's first open IP-address marketplace ("from parity... into superiority"), Cosmovisor-automated upgrades, and interchain accounts enabling Juno DAOs and Secret smart contracts to deploy on Akash without an Akash account. He flags Mainnet 4 as the prerequisite for "our next biggest update perhaps since the launch of the protocol" — the GPU marketplace — arguing society doesn't have enough GPUs for even immediate AI adoption, that text-to-image tools (Stable Diffusion, DALL-E 2) will unlock a new creative era, and that post-merge Ethereum miners will supply general-purpose GPUs ~20% cheaper (GPUs then trading ~5x MSRP). Ecosystem notes: Cloudmos deployment alerts (the "parking meter" churn problem), Chia's World Bank carbon-registry deal as institutional proof crypto can be green, the Evergreen home Chia miner that runs Akash software when idle, DoD usage gaining steam, USDC stable payments coming, and DHH's "leaving the cloud" post as validation — yielding his tagline "Akash lets you be sovereign at scale."

In the AMA he candidly explains flat active-application metrics: churn from users' escrow running dry, no stable payments, and poor fault-tolerance UX — and lays out a three-phase strategy (foundational security → cloud parity → superiority), predicting 1,000+ providers/regions making Akash "the most distributed cloud in the world," a Netflix-style breakout story driven by GPUs, ~25% of Cosmos already using Akash, and Akash as "the first commercial implementation of a supercloud" (Cornell, 2016). James covers Axelar's 27 connected chains, generalized message passing, one-click compute purchases with non-native assets, and the February Interop Summit in Miami.

### [gpu-economics] Society doesn't have enough GPUs for even immediate AI adoption

So I'm super bullish on Akash supporting GPUs... giving a world where we have AI that's much [more] affordable, much cheaper."

> "Akash lets you be sovereign at scale...
