## Summary

Short interview at the deAI Summit during TOKEN2049 Singapore (same footage also posted by Pundi AI as pz6mvbr-qYc). Greg introduces Akash as "the world's first decentralized supercloud" connecting underutilized resources with people who need them — 50–80% cheaper than Amazon, with scarce GPUs like H100s available cheaply. Asked about blockchain-AI integration, he offers a three-part framework: blockchains are great at resource coordination — (1) compute coordination, tapping underutilized data centers plus compute "in homes, game stations, your home appliances"; (2) data coordination, since AI systems can't access personal data sitting on our devices and crypto enables sovereignty plus safe sharing; and (3) model coordination — models are only hundreds of lines of code, and 40 million developers (not just OpenAI/Anthropic) could write them if intelligence were decentralized. He praises host Pundi X's data-classification work. On Bitcoin, he disclaims short-term predictions but gives a long-term thesis: runaway government spending and dollar devaluation ("30% of all the dollars in creation were done in the last three years") make scarce assets central; institutions like BlackRock are taking large positions; "Bitcoin is the sea and we're all the boats."

### [local-compute] Untapped compute in homes, game stations, home appliances
> "There's a lot of underutilized compute out there that's just not coordinated. We have underutilized compute data centers, we have compute in homes, game stations, your home appliances and whatnot... and decentralized systems like a blockchain can be used — and is being used right now, Akash is a primary example — to bring that to people."
> — [00:00:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0WXiGaybt0M&t=46s)

**Context:** First pillar of his blockchain-AI coordination framework; his recurring home-compute supply thesis.

### [decentralized-ai] Crypto enables data sovereignty for AI
> "We have a lot of data — you and I, we own our own data — and the AI systems don't have access to all the data that's sitting in our own devices. Is there a good and easy and safe way to share this data? That's where crypto does an incredible job in allowing for sovereignty at the same time access to your data."
> — [00:01:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0WXiGaybt0M&t=89s)

**Context:** Second pillar: data coordination; personal data on personal devices as the untapped input to AI.

### [open-source-ai] 40 million developers could write AI models
> "A few companies write models, like OpenAIs of the world, Anthropics of the world, but really there are 40 million developers that could essentially write models. Models are not a lot of code — 600 lines or a thousand lines... so decentralizing that intelligence to the people using blockchain is a phenomenal way."
> — [00:02:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0WXiGaybt0M&t=134s)

**Context:** Third pillar: model coordination — democratizing model authorship beyond a few labs.

Bitcoin is definitely turning out to be the hedge against this runaway inflation — considering that 30% of all the dollars in creation were done in the last three years."
