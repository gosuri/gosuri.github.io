## Summary

An Akash Network community interview with Anthony (of Moultrie), a Marine Corps veteran with an NSA/electronic-warfare background, about winning first and second place at a US Defense Department (Marine Corps "Bravo") hackathon. His team built an application for compressing, distributing, and destroying cryptographic keys, using Secret Network for private computation and Akash for censorship-resistant hosting accessed through a wallet-address proxy. He mentions the first DoD website running on Akash (a client-side-encrypted Marine unit public-access site), intent to move the product to the NSA, and interest in Secret 2.0's homomorphic encryption for Akash deployments. Greg Osuri does not speak in this clip; all substantive statements are Anthony's.
