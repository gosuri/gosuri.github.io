## Summary

An "Akash Analysis" clip (speaker uncredited; consistent with Greg's recurring arguments and "we" authority over Akash strategy) on incentives and product-market fit. He argues Akash grew organically from the start, unlike platforms that incentivize early behaviors before PMF: incentivizing pre-PMF ruins your data — you can't tell why people use the platform, and when incentives die the growth dies. PMF is "a long and slow road... no shortcuts"; Akash's PMF exists "but it's not strong enough yet," so incentives are reserved for post-PMF, which is why there are no extra provider/tenant incentives — current programs (Akash Accelerator, community fund) target builders instead. He teases that "on-chain incentives are coming" and will open "a new era for Akash," but only once the team is confident in PMF.
