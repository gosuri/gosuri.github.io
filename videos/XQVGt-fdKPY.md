## Summary

Akash Weekly Twitter Spaces hosted by Nadia Bajuelo, with Greg Osuri's updates followed by guest Solomon G (product manager of the Geeks Pics NFT collection and Lorem Ipsum metaverse on Secret Network, hosted on Akash). Greg covers SF Tech Week, the Terraform provider plugin (~800 downloads), new partner Mask (web3 browser with VPN) and Orbit ("Urbit") node hosting enabled by upcoming IP leases, Mainnet 4 one week out (delayed by orphaned-payment bugs), and jailed validators holding ~3.5M delegated AKT. The historically significant part is Greg's earliest extended GPU/generative-AI pitch: he describes using AI for his own cartooning, predicts feature-length AI-made films "very soon," claims AI model complexity (and GPU demand) is doubling every 3-4 months, notes A100s selling at 2-3x retail ($23-30k), and says ~$19B of GPUs bought for Ethereum mining now sit idle for Akash to unlock at lower cost — GPUs are the next big update after Mainnet 4. He muses that "machines will be able to schedule other machines." In the AMA he quantifies training costs (Stable Diffusion ~$600k, maybe $400-500k on Akash), explains why GPU users fit Akash (new to cloud, care only about performance and price), calls Stable Diffusion the first open-source AI model anyone can run on their own GPUs (vs closed OpenAI/Google), and predicts a convergence of web3 and generative AI. He also details planned USDC stable payments with a ~20% take-fee that buys and burns AKT, and an AuthZ custodial-wallet design he claims makes Akash "the only protocol that has figured out" compliant fiat/credit-card payments.

## Predictions & Notable Claims

### [other] Feature-length AI-generated films are coming very soon
> "Using AI, folks right now are creating not only drawings but animations. Very soon you'll be able to see a full feature-length film made with AI — and a lot of this means so much compute power."
> — [00:08:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XQVGt-fdKPY&t=521s)

**Context:** November 2022 (weeks before ChatGPT's release); Greg describes reviving his own cartooning with image generators as evidence generative AI found product-market fit.

### [gpu-economics] GPU demand is doubling every four months
> "The complexity of AI machines with all these amazing new applications is doubling every four months — that means the need for GPUs is doubling every four months... it's impossible to even get an Nvidia A100 GPU chip right now. They're selling double or maybe even triple the retail... $23,000 to $30,000, if you can get them."
> — [00:09:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XQVGt-fdKPY&t=566s)

**Context:** Justifying the GPU roadmap pivot; he repeats the "doubling every three to four months" figure at [00:37:05].

### [gpu-economics] $19B of idle Ethereum-mining GPUs to unlock
> "There are about 19 billion dollars of GPUs that have been purchased and deployed for eth mining that's just sitting idle... we are gearing up to have GPU support as soon as possible. I'm super excited to be able to unlock this GPU supply and offer it to the world at a significantly lower cost."
> — [00:10:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XQVGt-fdKPY&t=609s)

**Context:** Post-merge supply thesis: repurpose stranded proof-of-work GPUs for generative AI training and inference; GPU support is the next big update after Mainnet 4.

### [ai-agents] Machines will schedule other machines
> "Akash being a sovereign compute platform, it doesn't deny anybody compute. Tomorrow when you have machines, they'll be able to schedule other machines. I think we're going to see a very interesting world, and Akash is here to support the new world."
> — [00:10:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XQVGt-fdKPY&t=654s)

**Context:** Early (2022) articulation of the permissionless machine-to-machine compute economy later central to his AI-agent framing.

### [gpu-economics] Training Stable Diffusion: ~$600k, cheaper on Akash
> "A protocol like Stable Diffusion, or a model — it costs about six hundred thousand dollars to get a basic training... with Akash we expect that to be, I don't know, four hundred thousand dollars, 400 to 500,000 — still a lot of money saving. So HPC makes a lot of sense for us... there's just enormous amounts of GPUs just sitting there idle."
> — [00:34:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XQVGt-fdKPY&t=2097s)

**Context:** AMA on target customers; adds that GPU users are new cloud users who care about performance and price, not traditional cloud features — a better fit for Akash.

### [open-source-ai] Bullish on Stable Diffusion as the first open-source AI model

> — [00:38:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XQVGt-fdKPY&t=2311s)

**Context:** Points to the emerging ecosystem of Stable Diffusion hosting/prompt services finding centralized cloud too expensive; ties open-source models to self-hosted compute.

### [decentralized-ai] Web3 and generative AI will converge

**Context:** Answer on fiat payments and tokenomics; he adds at [00:47:51] that with the AuthZ custodial-wallet design "we are the only protocol, as far as I can tell, that has figured this out" for compliant credit-card payments.
