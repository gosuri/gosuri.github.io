## Summary

A segment from the Akash Accelerate 2025 keynote introducing Star Bonds, "a new model of fractional on-chain ownership for compute infrastructure" answering "how do we fund and govern a cloud with no single entity controls?" Mechanics: anyone reserves a Star Bond, a node keeper provisions and operates the star node, AI workloads run, and revenue flows back to holders. Quantified pitch: up to 6.4x ROI in high-demand scenarios, only 25% principal at risk in low-demand cases, IRR rising with early-tier discounts from 36.7% to 44.8%; discounts for anyone with over 100 AKT staked (deeper/longer stake, better tier). First issuance "Star Cluster Alpha" would release ~75,000 bonds at $1,000 par value. Framed as "not speculative crypto" but "productive digital assets tied to real-world AI infrastructure" — "capital formation for the age of permissionless AI." (Keynote segment; presenter consistent with Greg, who announced Star Bonds at Accelerate 2025.)
