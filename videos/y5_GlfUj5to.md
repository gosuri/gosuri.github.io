## Summary

Akash Weekly Twitter Spaces ("Building through the bear market"), hosted by Adam Wozny, introducing events manager Nadia Bajuelo, then a long Greg Osuri update and AMA weeks after the Terra/Luna crash. Greg covers: the rocky 15-hour Mainnet 3 upgrade (a bug in the auto-inflation module forced its removal) which nonetheless proved deployed apps keep running when the chain halts; a major refactor paying down seven years of technical debt by breaking the monolithic provider daemon into microservices; upcoming remote-signer support in the Omnibus package so validators can keep keys off-network; and the IP marketplace (fixed ports/unique IPs) that will finally let Solana and Bitcoin nodes run on Akash. On economics: AKT inflation (~23-25%, ~30% staking APR) is running ~11M tokens above white-paper estimates and needs a lowering proposal; he weighs cutting inflation versus a ~50% community tax redistributed to long-term stakers to curb sell pressure from Osmosis LPs. He announces a deliberately constrained AKT stablecoin design (deployment-payments only, no send function, holding penalties), explicitly avoiding Terra-style algorithmic shortcuts. GPUs: spec-ready with ~20 customers waiting, framed as something "no one has done" for general-purpose scheduling. Overclock (28 full-time, hiring 20% more while Coinbase lays off) has 2-3 years of runway. In the AMA he lays out the enterprise path: product-market fit and provider profitability first, then 300-500 providers giving broader geo-presence than Amazon, 90% lower cost, credit-card onboarding via AuthZ-hosted wallets, and eventually web2 SMBs; hype projects will die this cycle while metric-driven builders thrive. He also predicts SEC enforcement will target DeFi, not cloud businesses like Akash.
