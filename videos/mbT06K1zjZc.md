## Summary

Second half of a Crypto Rich deep-dive interview (July 2021). Greg explains AKT token mechanics: per-block streaming micropayments and escrow accounts, proof-of-stake security (he cites an ~84% staking ratio, "highest in the ecosystem"), and a planned inflation-funded subsidy mechanism to keep costs low if supply outstrips demand. Platform stats of the moment: mainnet platform live ~4 months, ~150 application deploys/day, ~140+ long-running apps, ~70% fill rate with capacity about to double or triple. He describes marketplace success as providers leaving "with an empty basket." Roadmap items: Gravity DEX, Terra stablecoin pair via IBC, partnerships with Althea (decentralized internet/5G that would run its 5G cores on Akash at a third the cost) and Agoric (smart contracts enabling multi-currency settlement so, e.g., Sentinel dVPN nodes could run on Akash paid in DVPN tokens). He argues Cosmos's lack of shared security creates a high barrier to entry and thus high-quality, utility-first projects versus ERC-20 liquidity-first tokens. The standout section is his early GPU thesis: GPUs are AI's biggest cost (citing an Andreessen Horowitz article that ~20% of AI companies' margins go to Amazon for GPUs), enormous idle GPU supply exists — including "GPUs in your Macintosh that are not used" and Ethereum's mining fleet about to be freed by proof-of-stake — with an Akash GPU marketplace targeted within ~6 months as "the holy grail." Closes on grants and censorship-resistant platforms.

## Predictions & Notable Claims

### [gpu-economics] GPUs are AI's biggest cost — ~20% of AI companies' margins go to Amazon
> "A big feature that's upcoming is GPUs. For us, GPUs are one of the most exciting things for me, because today the biggest cost for AI is GPUs. Andreessen Horowitz even wrote an amazing article recently looking at their portfolio companies... this GPU cost, which is about 20% of margins — so 20% of the money, either investment or revenue, these AI companies make goes to Amazon, which is a lot."
> — [00:13:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mbT06K1zjZc&t=805s)

**Context:** Announcing GPUs as Akash's next big feature in mid-2021, well before the AI boom. Captions garble "Andreessen Horowitz" as "in recent horowitz."

### [local-compute] Idle GPUs everywhere — "GPUs in your Macintosh that are not used" and Ethereum's soon-to-be-free mining fleet
> "There are GPUs in your Macintosh that are not used — there's lots of GPUs out there. Data centers themselves have enormous GPUs, and more importantly you have Ethereum, which is proof of work transitioning to proof of stake — this entire GPU infrastructure is going to be free. So a lot of GPUs around, but there's no way for these GPUs to hit the market."
> — [00:14:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mbT06K1zjZc&t=848s)

**Context:** The supply-side case for a GPU marketplace: consumer machines, data centers, and post-Merge Ethereum miners all hold stranded GPU capacity. Early statement of the home-hardware-earning thesis.

### [gpu-economics] GPU marketplace in ~6 months will be "the holy grail"
> "That should be out in another six months or so if everything goes right — and turns out making a multi-tenancy marketplace for GPUs is not the easiest thing... if you can run TensorFlow on Akash — you can right now, but with GPUs — I think that's going to be a holy grail."
> — [00:15:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mbT06K1zjZc&t=931s)

**Context:** Timeline forecast for Akash GPU support; he also relays a researcher's claim that ~90% of GPU cost in self-driving development is (non-latency-sensitive) debugging/replay, making geographically distributed GPUs viable.

### [cloud-decentralization] Akash will offer more managed services than Amazon's ~250
> "Akash is going to come up with managed services, but we're going to do that in a decentralized way like we've done with compute. It's a very hard problem to solve... it's going to take a while, but when that comes — Amazon has about 250 managed services, but Akash will have a lot more than that because of the decentralized permissionless marketplace."
> — [00:12:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mbT06K1zjZc&t=762s)

**Context:** Explaining that managed services (where AWS "makes most of the money") are the long-term roadmap beyond bare-bones container hosting.

### [crypto-depin] The ultimate interoperable future: services paying for compute in their own tokens
> "You don't have to go give your credit card, name and your address to some Amazon or somebody to run a dVPN... rather just use your dVPN tokens that you gain to pay for the cloud computing service. That's the ultimate sort of interoperable future that I envisioned."
> — [00:11:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mbT06K1zjZc&t=719s)

**Context:** Describing Agoric smart contracts enabling multi-currency settlement on Akash — a fully self-sustaining, anonymous ecosystem where Sentinel dVPN nodes run on Akash paid in DVPN.

### [crypto-depin] IBC will be adopted far beyond Cosmos
> "I think we're going to see Cosmos interoperability, or the IBC — inter-blockchain communication — be adopted way beyond just Cosmos... Once that happens, you're going to create a very powerful ecosystem that's built with utility at core, and with a growing adoption around that utility, all intercommunicating in a frictionless way."
