## Summary

An operational community livestream announcing Phase 2 of the Akashian Challenge incentivized testnet, co-hosted by Greg and newly hired VP of Product Jack Zampolin (ex-head of product at Cosmos/Tendermint, who shipped the Cosmos Hub and IBC). Phase 1 recap: the largest Cosmos testnet since Game of Stakes — 211 teams, 91+ active validators, 84 online at genesis, surviving a sustained DDoS attack and an AWS outage. Phase 2 runs three weeks on internally operated validators to rapid-fire test the decentralized exchange (order book) side: week one "capture the orders" (providers bid on team-submitted deployments via akashctl), week two throughput testing scored on lease hours, week three large-scale multi-provider deployments, staging for Phase 3's real-application deployments (Matrix, Jitsi, WordPress-style open-source catalog, plus DeFi frontends like Uniswap/Compound). Jack explains he remains involved in Cosmos Hub governance and that Akash will push UX contributions upstream to the Cosmos SDK; IBC support is planned to enable stablecoin payments for compute. Notable dated moments: mainnet is estimated "within the next month to six weeks" (Jack; Mainnet 1 actually arrived September 2020), Solana is in discussions to run its nodes and testnets on Akash, and the Super Mini home compute device sold out its first 300 units in about four weeks. Greg's video drops mid-stream; giveaways and a Super Mini raffle close it out. Light on predictions — mostly testnet logistics.
