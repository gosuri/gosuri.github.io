## Summary

An "Akash Analysis" clip (speaker consistent with Greg — he plugs his own upcoming Messari Mainnet talk) on why decentralization matters. Centralized cloud is "basically a kill switch," and citing a16z's "true cost of cloud" analysis, he argues ~50% of the cost/margins of every cloud service we use — Notion, Asana, Netflix — goes to the big cloud providers: "half of every dollar we pay to cloud services goes to Uncle Jeff," an invisible rent-seeking model. Decentralization thus matters both for censorship resistance and for reducing global costs in high-inflation times. He leans on the US Department of Defense website running on Akash as a proof point ("if Akash is good enough for a US Department of Defense website, Akash is good enough for a lot of folks"), praises the military as "the purest form of government" and his preferred first government user, and argues such proof points matter for crypto policy perception.
