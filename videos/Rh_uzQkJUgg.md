## Summary

Conference talk/demo at Akash Accelerate 2025 by the founders of Passage, a virtual-events startup that streams Unreal Engine 5 experiences to any browser using GPUs rented on Akash. They say Akash cut their GPU costs by ~70%, cite closed-beta traction (80 paying teams, 850+ events, ~2,500 GPU hours, clients like MIT, Capital Records, Amazon), and close with a live audience demo. Greg Osuri does not appear or speak.
