## Summary

A tight five-minute interview segment (Bigeye Studios, November 2024) in which Greg explains why Akash wants to borrow Bitcoin's security through Babylon. He opens by framing AI as the most important emerging layer of daily life — he uses AI models "to raise my kid," do his work, and process every email — and argues that infrastructure this important must be secure enough to "withstand a nation state attack." Akash's proof-of-stake security is good enough for go-to-market but carries a large security budget and falls short of Bitcoin's guarantees, so bridging Bitcoin security (and Bitcoin payments) to Akash via Babylon is the plan: phased, starting with archival transactions and moving toward real-time transactions as Babylon matures. The big thesis: just as Bitcoin decoupled money from the state, decentralized compute secured by Bitcoin can decouple intelligence from the state, turning Bitcoin from a pure money chain into the security layer for an "unstoppable cloud" that fuels AI — host a model, pay in sats, secured by Bitcoin, with Akash as the application layer. He acknowledges Bitcoin maximalists may resist extending Bitcoin beyond money.
