## Summary

Akash community livestream hosted by Adam Wozney (head of community) with Andrew Mello (Akash head of mining) and John Michael Hands (Chia VP of storage), following the Akash-Chia partnership announcement. Hands explains Chia's proof-of-space-and-time consensus and its ~300,000 farmers, and frames Akash as inexpensive "plotting as a service" that dodges cloud egress fees; Mello demos creating a Chia plot via Akashlytics (wallet setup, SDL templates, bids around 26-92 uakt, escrow, provider attributes like `chia-plotting: true`) and pitches idle plotting rigs becoming Akash providers. Notable claims come from the guests, not Greg: Hands estimates Akash plotting at roughly one-fifth the cost of the cheapest US plotting service, and Mello foreshadows persistent storage on Akash making plot storage on the d-web advantageous. Greg Osuri does not appear in this video.
