## Summary

Segment from an "Ask Akash" community call (November 2022) where an investor asks why Akash's active-application metrics have been flat for a year. Greg says he's not concerned: metrics this early are not quantitatively meaningful, and flatness reflects churn rather than no adoption. The biggest reason people leave is embarrassingly mundane — the prepaid AKT escrow runs out (about 15–20 days per 5 AKT for a blog) and deployments die, making users think Akash is broken. Other inhibitors: no stable payments (AKT volatility on both tenant and provider side) and users not knowing how to deploy multi-region for fault tolerance. Fixes underway: Cloudmos alerts, authorized spend enabling custodial credit-card payments, USDC stable payments, and Overclock's opinionated "console" product due in three to four weeks. He frames the roadmap as a "parity game" with traditional cloud (stable payments, observability, automation, identity, access control — "next few quarters," full Fortune-50-ready parity "next few years"), but says GPUs will flip Akash from parity to superiority: the first open market for GPUs, supplied by post-Merge proof-of-work miners, much cheaper than cloud. He draws the Amazon/Netflix analogy — cloud growth is non-linear and driven by a marquee-customer story — and predicts GPUs will deliver Akash's Netflix moment.

## Predictions & Notable Claims

### [gpu-economics] GPUs move Akash from parity to superiority via the first open GPU market
> "So far Akash is just really trying to create a parity game... can we give capabilities of the current cloud provider... With GPUs we'll start seeing superiority. That means for the first time you'll see Akash deliver something the current cloud does not have, which is an open market for GPUs... offering GPUs for [way] cheaper and the ubiquitous amount of GPUs, all the way from miners... after the merge."
> — [00:07:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oB45bmSF4uY&t=474s)

**Context:** November 2022, before Akash's GPU launch; anticipates post-Ethereum-merge mining GPUs flooding the marketplace.

### [gpu-economics] GPUs will be Akash's Netflix moment
> "A critical event that took Amazon to the next level is Netflix... that one story put Amazon on a different planet. Similarly, Akash — the story is going to be one of the users on Akash... doing incredible things to get a competitive advantage over their competitors that are using the traditional cloud, and those stories are going to get Akash... exponential. I think we're gonna see GPUs really deliver that Netflix for Akash."
> — [00:10:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oB45bmSF4uY&t=604s)

**Context:** Explaining why flat 2022 metrics don't matter: cloud adoption is non-linear and needs a marquee use case, which he predicts GPUs will provide.

### [cloud-decentralization] Cloud parity is a multi-year journey
> "All these features are required for us to get parity [with] the cloud and then comfortably go to a big major Fortune 10 company or Fortune 50 company and be like, hey, move all your workloads [to] Akash, because now you have all the capabilities that you need to run. That's not the next few months — it's going to be next few years."
> — [00:12:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oB45bmSF4uY&t=775s)

**Context:** Tempering a questioner's optimism about timelines; stable payments, observability, automation, identity, and access control were still on the roadmap, with GPUs next after Mainnet 4.
