## Summary

Closing livestream for the Akashian Challenge Phase 3 testnet (December 2020, three months after mainnet 1 launch). Greg recaps testnet results: ~142–149 validators, ~3,600 app deploys in week two, ~460 unique deployers (which he compares favorably to Polkadot's ~430 monthly active developers), and 50+ community-contributed apps in the Awesome Akash repo — from DeFi frontends (Uniswap, Serum, ThorChain) to block explorers and the Ghost anonymous blogging platform. CTO Adam Bozanich details bugs the testnet surfaced (OOM-killed deployments, SDL confusion, bid/scheduler inventory desync) and demos a Big Dipper block-explorer deployment via CLI. Company updates: AKT hit an all-time high of $0.96, 88.5% of supply staked, and Akash ranked second in trending searches on CoinGecko. The Q&A covers inflation reduction governance, the mainnet 1 → mainnet 2 transition, GUI plans (community protocol clients, Keplr-based non-custodial hosting), why they chose Cosmos/Tendermint over Ethereum (CryptoKitties congestion killed their 2017–2018 prototype plans), Solana support (Greg notes he did Solana's first on-stage interview and Akash hosts the Serum DEX UI), private/hybrid deployments, and 2021 plans centered on mainnet 2, economics, and IBC interoperability. Asked what Akash looks like in 2031, the answer floated is a $300B market — "it'll be like the clouds, it'll be everywhere." Greg closes arguing Akash solves a real problem: "cheaper, better, faster compute."
