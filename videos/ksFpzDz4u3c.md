## Summary

An Akash team explainer presented by Alani (not Greg) announcing Akash as the preferred decentralized cloud hosting solution for the Polygon ecosystem. Covers how moving dApps to Akash eliminates reliance on a single cloud provider (switching providers is just closing a deployment and creating a new lease), notes over 70% of Polygon's ~700 dApps are DeFi, and details the "Polygonathon" incentive program: 20 AKT hosting credits for Polygon projects (2-3 months of hosting, 4 cores/16 GB RAM), bounty matching up to 100 AKT, and up to $100k from the Akash developer grant program.
