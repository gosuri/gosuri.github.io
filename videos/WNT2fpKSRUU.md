## Summary

An "Ask Akash" Q&A clip (speaker uncredited; consistent with Greg — speaks for Overclock Labs strategy and regulatory posture) on when take fees/take income (a share of hosting fees paid to AKT stakers) will ship. The blocker isn't engineering — "it's taking a percentage of hosting fees and giving back to stakers, it's not a complicated piece of software" — but US regulation: take income could be considered a security, a "regulatory gray hole," even though the proposal isn't very different from Ethereum's staking income. The team is waiting for regulatory clarity and "looking at creative ways to not classify Akash a security," noting the US environment is "really crazy... for anything that promises profits of some sort." He suggests it would be better if the capability came from the community rather than the Overclock Labs team, as Akash decentralizes.
