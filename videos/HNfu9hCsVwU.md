## Summary

Russian-language interview ("Why is it expensive to launch a startup in centralized clouds?") with host Dmitry Krishtal of S3XYBTC, recorded about a month after Akash Mainnet 2 (May 2021). Greg's answers are presented as a Russian voiceover dub, so quotes below are the translator's Russian rendering of his English answers, with English back-translations. Greg pitches Akash as the world's first independent, censorship-resistant, permissionless open-source cloud versus the Amazon/Google/Microsoft/Alibaba oligopoly: ~8x-10x cheaper, no KYC, and no one — "not even me" — able to remove an application. He explains the "Airbnb for cloud" analogy (Hilton = predictable AWS, Airbnb = choice among ~8 million data centers worldwide), the third-party audit/attestation system for provider trust, plans for a Uniswap-like web interface, and a philosophy of doing few things well at launch (iPhone without copy-paste, Twitter without retweets), including deliberately not building storage. Early traction: 30+ apps in a month, ~80 developers (vs. Polkadot's 400), users running Akash-on-Akash, Monero mining, a space-telescope workload, and DeFi projects like Serum. Most notable for the archive: his 2021 forecast that Ethereum's move to proof-of-stake would strand a huge GPU fleet, letting Akash acquire GPUs "at half price" to serve the then-nascent AI market — an early, dated articulation of his GPU-economics-for-AI thesis. He also cites the cloud market growing from $300B toward $400B and positions Akash as "the only open-source cloud."

### [gpu-economics] Ethereum's PoS switch strands GPU infrastructure — a huge AI opportunity
> "Эфир переходит на Proof of Stake, а значит, огромная инфраструктура графических адаптеров, которая поддерживает эфир, станет никому не нужной. Это предоставляет огромную возможность для рынка ИИ, искусственного интеллекта. Мы строим инфраструктуру, ориентированную именно на этот рынок."
> ("Ethereum is moving to Proof of Stake, which means the enormous GPU infrastructure supporting Ethereum will no longer be needed by anyone. This presents a huge opportunity for the AI market. We are building infrastructure aimed precisely at that market.")
> — [00:15:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HNfu9hCsVwU&t=947s)

**Context:** May 2021, over a year before the Merge; he adds the GPU flood "will solve the problem of their cost." An early dated statement of Akash's GPU-for-AI pivot.

Когда произойдет переход, мы хотим быть готовы к работе с рынком графических процессоров. Мы считаем, что сможем купить их за полцены, а для рынка искусственного интеллекта это очень важно."

When the transition happens, we want to be ready to work with the GPU market. We believe we'll be able to buy them at half price, and for the artificial intelligence market that is very important.")

**Context:** Naming the GPU marketplace as his #2 roadmap priority after usability; quantified forecast on GPU prices post-Merge.
