## Summary

An Akash team explainer presented by Melanie (not Greg) introducing authz (authorized spend) for Testnet 3: users authorize another wallet to spend a set number of their tokens strictly on Akash deployment activities — the recipient can't use them for other transactions. This lets large teams collaborate on deployments without risky shared wallets and lets new community members deploy without faucet access. Ends with the Testnet 3 signup/KYC workflow and the $200,000 prize pool.
