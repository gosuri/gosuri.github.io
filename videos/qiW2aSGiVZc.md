## Summary

An Akash team explainer presented by Alani (not Greg) introducing the inflationary decay curve for Testnet 3. Previously AKT inflation was corrected manually via governance proposals, which occasionally let inflation drift out of ideal ranges — bad for trust and token stability. The automatic curve mechanism autocorrects when inflation deviates from plan, removing human intervention. Ends with the Testnet 3 signup/KYC workflow and the $200,000 prize pool.
