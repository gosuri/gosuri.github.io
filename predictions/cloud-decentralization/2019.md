### 2019-08-06 — Akash as a new operating system for the internet tapping 85% unused capacity

_Overcoming the Challenges of Distributed Networks with Greg Osuri CEO Overclock Labs & Akash Network (Techpost)_

> "We believe the foundation should be scalable, frictionless and free of censorship, and we believe in the unstoppable web. What we're doing with Akash is essentially creating a new operating system for the Internet, and how we're doing that is by tapping into unused 85% capacity."
> — [00:03:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WKvrKWdc9OA&t=182s)

**Context:** Opening pitch; he completes the figure in the next breath — "in 8.2 million data centers" — describing Akash as a peer-to-peer network for "planetary scale applications."

### 2019-08-06 — Cloud is built for hyperscale; Akash optimizes for thousands of nodes at a fraction of the cost

_Overcoming the Challenges of Distributed Networks with Greg Osuri CEO Overclock Labs & Akash Network (Techpost)_

> "Cloud wasn't quite cutting it for them in terms of performance because cloud is optimized for hyper scale... high-density data centers, but Akash doesn't have that limitation. Akash will optimize a global scale for thousands of nodes... by using Akash they could get cost down, I think it's 4x or 5x, than what they would otherwise pay on the cloud."
> — [00:08:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WKvrKWdc9OA&t=487s)

**Context:** Describing Cactus, a peer-to-peer music streaming app using relay architecture at the edge; next block adds "about 40 times more distribution than the cloud."
