As the founder of Akash Network and CEO of Overclock Labs, Greg has spent a career arguing that compute should belong to the people who use it. Before Akash he founded AngelHack, the world’s largest hackathon organization, with over 100,000 developers across 50 cities; he launched his career at IBM and later designed Kaiser Permanente’s first cloud architecture. He was instrumental in the passing of California’s first blockchain law, providing the first expert-witness testimony at the Senate.
